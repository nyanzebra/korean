## 아침을 먹고 있어요 #card #daily
?begin
### 뜻
- I'm having breakfast / I'm eating breakfast
### 예
- 지금 아침을 먹고 있어요
	- I'm having breakfast right now
- 아침을 먹고 있으니까 잠깐만 기다려요
	- I'm eating breakfast, wait a moment please
- 벌써 아침 먹었어?
	- Did you already have breakfast?
?end
