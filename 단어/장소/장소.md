## 이곳 / 곳 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- this place; here (이곳)
- place; spot (곳)

### 예
- 이곳은 아름다워요
  - This place is beautiful
- 이곳에 자주 와요
  - I come here often
- 여기는 유명한 곳이에요
  - This is a famous place
- 조용한 곳이에요
  - It is a quiet place

### Notes
- 곳 is a dependent noun used with a modifier: 조용한 곳 (a quiet place)

?end

## 개발하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to develop (a new product, technology, etc.)
- to develop (land or an area) for use

### 예
- 우리 회사는 새로운 앱을 개발했어요
  - Our company developed a new app
- 정부가 이 지역을 관광지로 개발하기로 했어요
  - The government decided to develop this area into a tourist destination

### Notes
- 개발하다: someone actively creates or builds something (transitive)
- 발전하다: something grows or advances by itself (intransitive)

<!--SR:!2027-02-12,306,228-->
?end

## 발전하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to develop (grow, advance, make progress)
- to improve over time

### 예
- 한국 경제가 빠르게 발전했어요
  - The Korean economy developed rapidly
- 이 도시는 앞으로 더 발전할 거예요
  - This city will develop even more in the future
- 한국어 실력이 많이 발전했네요
  - Your Korean has improved a lot

### Notes
- 발전하다: something grows or advances by itself (intransitive)
- 개발하다: someone actively creates or builds something (transitive)

?end
