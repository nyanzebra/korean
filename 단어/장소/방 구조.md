# 방 구조 (Room Structure)

## 마당 #card #housing #yard
?begin
### 뜻
- yard; garden (open area next to a house)
- walled courtyard; grounds
- situation, circumstances (metaphorical, as in -는 마당에)

### 예
- 집에 마당이 있어
  - There's a yard at the house
- 마당을 쓸고 있어
  - I'm sweeping the yard
- 아이들이 마당에서 놀고 있어
  - Children are playing in the yard
- 급한 마당에 의논할 여유가 없었어
  - In such an urgent situation, I didn't have time to discuss
- 이 마을을 떠나는 마당에 사람들에게 선물을 준비했다
  - As I was leaving this town, I prepared gifts for the people
- 마당에서 놀아요
  - I play in the yard
- 넓은 마당
  - Wide yard

### Notes
- Related: 뜰 (yard), 정원 (garden), 상황 (situation - metaphorical)

?end

## 천정 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- zenith (the highest point of the sky)
- sky-high (in 천정부지)

### 예
- 해가 천정에 떠 있어요
  - The sun is at its zenith
- 물가가 천정부지로 치솟고 있다
  - Prices are skyrocketing

### Notes
- For "ceiling" the standard word is 천장; 천정 is a nonstandard variant
- 천정부지 (天井不知): no end in sight, prices rising endlessly

?end
