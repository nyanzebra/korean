## 매진하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to sell out
- to be sold out
### 예
- 주말 표는 벌써 매진됐어요
  - The weekend tickets are already sold out
- 그 영화는 개봉하자마자 매진됐어요
  - That movie sold out as soon as it was released
### Notes
- Usually used passively: 매진되다 (be sold out)

?end
