## 볼일 좀 보고 오다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to go take care of some business (euphemism for going to the bathroom)
### 예
- 잠깐 볼일 좀 보고 올게요
  - I'll be right back, I need to take care of something (go to the bathroom)
- 볼일 좀 보고 왔어요
  - I went to the bathroom and came back
### Notes
- 볼일 (보다) also means "errands/business", depending on context

?end
