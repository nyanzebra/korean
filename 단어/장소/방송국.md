## 방청객 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- studio audience
- audience (at a live broadcast or show)
### 예
- 방청객들이 큰 소리로 박수를 쳤어요
  - The studio audience applauded loudly
- 방청객으로 그 프로그램 녹화에 참여했어요
  - I took part in the recording of that program as a studio audience member
?end

## 출연자 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- performer
- guest or cast member on a TV show

### 예
- 이번 주 출연자는 유명한 가수예요
  - This week's guest is a famous singer
- 출연자들이 무대에 올라왔어요
  - The performers came up on stage

?end

## 방송에 나오다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to appear on TV (or radio)

### 예
- 우리 동네 식당이 방송에 나왔어요
  - A restaurant in our neighborhood appeared on TV
- 내 친구가 방송에 나온다고 해서 같이 봤어요
  - My friend was going to be on TV, so we watched it together

?end

## 인기상을 타다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to win a popularity award (viewers' choice award)

### 예
- 그 배우는 올해 인기상을 탔어요
  - That actor won the popularity award this year
- 시청자들의 투표로 인기상을 타게 됐어요
  - He won the viewers' choice award through viewer votes

### Notes
- 상을 타다 = to win an award/prize

?end

## 초대 손님 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- special guest (on a TV show)

  - Today's special guest is a famous writer
