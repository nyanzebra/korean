## 손을 불에 데다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to burn one's hand (on fire/heat)

### 예
- 요리하다가 손을 불에 데었어요
  - I burned my hand while cooking
- 뜨거운 냄비에 손을 데지 않게 조심하세요
  - Be careful not to burn your hand on the hot pot

### Notes
- 데다 = to get burned by heat; 베다 = to get cut

?end
