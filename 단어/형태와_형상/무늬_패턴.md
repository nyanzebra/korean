# 무늬와 패턴 (Patterns and Stripes)

## 무늬 #card #pattern
?begin
### 뜻
- pattern
- design, motif
### 예
- 이 옷은 꽃무늬가 예뻐요
  - The flower pattern on this dress is pretty
- 벽지에 무늬가 있어요
  - The wallpaper has a pattern on it
### Notes
- 꽃무늬: flower pattern, 물방울무늬: polka dots, 체크무늬: checkered pattern
- Related: 패턴 (pattern, loanword), 줄무늬 (stripes)
?end

## 줄무늬 #card #pattern #stripe
?begin
### 뜻
- stripes, striped pattern
### 예
- 줄무늬 셔츠를 입었어요
  - I wore a striped shirt
- 호랑이는 몸에 줄무늬가 있어요
  - A tiger has stripes on its body
### Notes
- 줄: line, stripe (줄 + 무늬 = 줄무늬)
?end

## 조 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- group, team (組)
- article, clause (條)
- (counter) number of the group/article
### 예
- 우리는 같은 조예요
  - We're in the same group
- 1조, 2조, 3조...
  - group 1, group 2, group 3...
- 헌법 제1조
  - Article 1 of the Constitution
### Notes
- The earlier version of this card listed 조 as "stripe / mark on skin" with 줄/조 무늬 and 목에 조가 있다; that is not standard usage — "stripe" is 줄 / 줄무늬
- Same spelling is also used for a counting unit for trillion (兆): 1조 원
- Related: 팀 (team), 모둠 (group of students)
- See also: [[수와_양]] (other meaning: 조 兆 = trillion)
?end
