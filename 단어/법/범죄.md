## 위조 #card #crime #fake #illegal #counterfeit
?begin
### 뜻
- counterfeit
- fake
- forgery
### 예
- 위조 지폐를 사용한 혐의로 체포됐어요
  - He was arrested on suspicion of using counterfeit bills
- 그 여권은 위조된 것으로 밝혀졌어요
  - The passport turned out to be a forgery
### Notes
- 위조하다: to forge, to counterfeit
- Related: 변조 (alteration/tampering), 가짜 (fake)
?end

## 훔치다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to steal
- to rob
### 예
- 누가 제 지갑을 훔쳤어요.
  - Someone stole my wallet.
- 가게에서 물건을 훔치면 안 돼요.
  - You must not steal things from a store.
### Notes
- Past: 훔쳤어요
- Related: 도둑 (thief), 도둑맞다 (to have something stolen, see [[당한 것]]), 소매치기 (pickpocket)
?end
