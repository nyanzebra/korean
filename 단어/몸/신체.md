## 온몸 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- one's whole body

### 예
- 운동을 했더니 온몸이 쑤셔요
  - My whole body aches after exercising
- 그 소식을 듣고 온몸에 소름이 돋았어
  - I got goosebumps all over my body when I heard the news

### Notes
- Related: 몸 (body), 전신 (whole body, more formal)

?end

## 뒷모습 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- the view/appearance of someone from behind

### 예
- 멀어지는 그의 뒷모습을 바라봤어
  - I watched his figure from behind as he walked away
- 뒷모습만 봐도 누군지 알 수 있어요
  - I can tell who it is just by seeing them from behind

### Notes
- Related: 앞모습 (appearance from the front), 모습 (appearance)

?end

## 피곤하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be tired

- to be weary

### 예
- 너무 피곤해요
  - I'm so tired.

## 배고프다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be hungry
- to be starving

### 예
- 너무 배고파요
  - I'm so hungry.
- 배고파 죽겠어요
  - I'm starving to death.

### Notes
