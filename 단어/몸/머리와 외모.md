# 머리와 외모 (Hair and Appearance)

## 머리를 감다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to wash one's hair

### 예
- 아침마다 머리를 감아요
  - I wash my hair every morning
- 샴푸로 머리를 감았어요
  - I washed my hair with shampoo

?end

## 머리를 말리다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to dry one's hair

### 예
- 머리를 감고 드라이어로 말려요
  - After washing my hair, I dry it with a hair dryer
- 머리를 안 말리고 자면 감기에 걸려요
  - If you go to sleep without drying your hair, you'll catch a cold

?end

?begin
