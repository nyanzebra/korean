## 씹히다 #card #chewing #eating #teeth #physical
?begin
### 뜻
- to be chewed
- to be bitten accidentally (e.g. tongue)
- to chew (well)
### 예
- 밥을 먹다가 혀가 씹혔어요
  - I accidentally bit my tongue while eating
- 고기가 질겨서 잘 안 씹혀요
  - The meat is tough, so it doesn't chew well
?end

## 악물다 #card #physical #clench #bite #determination
?begin
### 뜻
- to clench (one's teeth)
- to grit one's teeth
### 예
- 이를 악물고 참았어요
  - I gritted my teeth and endured it
- 이를 악물고 끝까지 달렸어요
  - I ran to the end with gritted teeth
### Notes
- 이를 악물다 = to clench/grit one's teeth (also: to steel oneself)
?end
