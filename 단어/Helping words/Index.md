# Helping Words (보조 단어)

This folder contains structural and grammatical vocabulary that supports core vocabulary, including particles, conjunctions, onomatopoeia, counters, and directional expressions.

## Files in this folder:

- **Helping words.md** - General helping words and particles
- **관용구.md** - Idiomatic expressions and phrases
- **대명사.md** - Pronouns
- **말하기.md** - Speech and communication verbs
- **방향 동사.md** - Directional verbs
- **방향.md** - Directional words and concepts
- **부분 표현.md** - Partial and fractional expressions
- **부사.md** - Adverbs
- **수와 카운터.md** - Numbers and counters
- **시간 개념.md** - Time concepts
- **시간 표현.md** - Time expressions
- **위치 표현.md** - Location and position expressions
- **의성어.md** - Onomatopoeia
- **의성어_및_표현.md** - Onomatopoeia and expressions
- **의태어.md** - Mimetic words (descriptive sounds and movements)
- **접속사.md** - Conjunctions
- **표현.md** - General expressions
- **표현_및_언어.md** - Expressions and language
- **필수 동사.md** - Essential verbs
- **필수 형용사.md** - Essential adjectives
