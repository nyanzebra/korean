## 이제 #card #time #now #finally #from-now-on
?begin
### 뜻
- now
- finally
- from now on
### 예

	- It's time to go home now.
- 이제부터 열심히 공부할 거예요.

## 간만 #card #time #while #duration #colloquial
?begin
### 뜻
- ebb and flow
- flux
- it's been a while
### 예
- 간만에 바람 좀 쐬면서 걸을까 한다
	- It's been a while since I've had some fresh air
- 간만에 친구들을 만나서 즐거웠어요.
	- I enjoyed meeting my friends for the first time in a while.
### Notes
- 'It's been a while' sense: 간만에 is a colloquial shortening of 오랜만에 / 오래간만에
- 'Ebb and flow' sense: 간만 (干滿) = low and high tide, 간만의 차 = tidal range
?end

## 우리가 마지막으로 직접 만난 지 오래되었다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- it has been a long time since we last met in person
### 예
- 우리가 마지막으로 직접 만난 지 오래되었으니 이번 주말에 한번 보자.
	- It's been a long time since we last met in person, so let's meet this weekend.
- 우리가 마지막으로 직접 만난 지 벌써 2년이 넘었네요.
	- It has already been over two years since we last met in person.
### Notes
- Pattern: -(으)ㄴ 지 + time + 되다 / 오래되다 (it has been ... since ...)
### Tags
#time-phrase #long-time #meeting #phrase #duration

?end
