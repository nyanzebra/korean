## 스스로 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- oneself
- itself
### 예
- 그는 스스로 목숨을 끊었다
	- he ended his own life
- 그것은 스스로 화를 청하는 짓이다
	- With that he is inviting disaster of his own accord.

	- style oneself as a great scholar.

## 자기 #card #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- self
- oneself
- own

### 예
- 그녀는 자기가 소설 속의 한 인물이 된 것 같은 느낌이 들었다
  - She felt as if she were a character in a novel.
- 그는 친구 앞에서의 자기 체면치레를 위해 그것에 관하여 전혀 모르는 체했다
  - He pretended to be ignorant of it in order to save face in the presence of his friends.
- 아무도 반론을 펴지 않자 그는 우쭐해져서 자기 주장을 마구 늘어놓았다
  - Since nobody contradicted him, he just complacently rattled about his idea[theory].
- 자길 사랑해야 해요
  - You must love yourself
- 자길 돌봐야 해요
  - You need to take care of yourself

### Notes
- Difference between 스스로 and 자기: https://korean.stackexchange.com/questions/3459/what-is-the-difference-between-%EC%8A%A4%EC%8A%A4%EB%A1%9C-and-%EC%9E%90%EA%B8%B0
- 자길 = 자기를 (contraction)

## 나 #card #pronoun #I #first-person #self
?begin
### 뜻
- I
- me
### 예
- 나는 학생이야.
	- I'm a student.
- 이 책은 나한테 너무 어려워.
	- This book is too hard for me.
### Notes
- Informal 'I'; the humble form is 저. With the subject marker: 나 + 가 → 내가 (저 + 가 → 제가)
?end

## 너 #card #pronoun #you #second-person #informal
?begin
### 뜻
- you (informal)
### 예
- 너 어디 가?
	- Where are you going?
- 이건 너를 위한 선물이야.
	- This is a present for you.
### Notes
- Only for close friends or people younger than you. With the subject marker: 너 + 가 → 네가
?end

## 우리 #card #pronoun #we #first-person #plural #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- we
- us
- our
### 예
- 우리는 같은 반 친구예요.
	- We are classmates.
- 우리 집에 놀러 오세요.
	- Please come over to our house.
### Notes
- Koreans often say 우리 where English says 'my' for family and groups: 우리 엄마, 우리 학교
?end

## 그 #card #demonstrative #that #determiner #reference
?begin
### 뜻
- that
- the
### 예
- 그 사람을 알아요?
	- Do you know that person?
- 그 책은 어제 샀어요.
	- I bought that book yesterday.
### Notes
- Refers to something already mentioned or close to the listener (이 = near me, 그 = near you / known, 저 = far from both)
?end

## 저 #card #demonstrative #that #far #reference
?begin
### 뜻
- that (over there)
### 예
- 저 건물이 우리 학교예요.
	- That building over there is our school.
- 저 사람이 누구예요?
	- Who is that person over there?
### Notes
- 저 is also the humble 'I' (저는, 제가) — different word, same form
?end

## 거기 #card #location #there #place #demonstrative #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- there
- that place

### 예
- 거기서 기다려 주세요.
  - Please wait there.
- 어제 거기에 처음 가 봤어요.
  - I went there for the first time yesterday.
- 거길 가고 싶어요
  - I want to go there
- 거길 찾고 있어요
  - I'm looking for that place

### Notes
- 여기 = here, 거기 = there (near the listener / already mentioned), 저기 = over there
- 거길 = 거기를 (contraction)

## 누구 #card #question #who #person #interrogative
?begin
### 뜻
- who
- someone
### 예
- 누구를 기다려요?
	- Who are you waiting for?
- 방에 누구 있어요?
	- Is anyone (someone) in the room?
### Notes
- With the subject marker: 누구 + 가 → 누가
?end

## 무엇 #card #question #what #thing #interrogative
?begin
### 뜻
- what
- something
### 예
- 지금 무엇을 하고 있어요?
	- What are you doing now?
- 무엇인가 이상한 소리가 들려요.
	- I hear something strange.
### Notes
- In speech 무엇 is usually shortened to 뭐 (무엇을 → 뭘)
?end
