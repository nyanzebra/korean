# 의성어 (Onomatopoeia & Sound Words)

## 웅성거리다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to murmur, to buzz (of a crowd)
- to hum with excited voices
### 예
- 군중들의 웅성거림이 단속적으로 들려 왔다
	- The racket made by the mob could be heard intermittently
- 웅성거리는 대화 소리.
	- a buzz of conversation
### Tags
#sound #onomatopoeia #crowd-noise #murmuring #buzzing #humming
?end

## 엉엉 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- wah wah (crying sound)
- sobbing
### 예
- 엉엉 울다
	- to cry/sob loudly
- 아이가 엉엉 울면서 엄마를 찾았다.
	- The child was sobbing loudly, calling for its mother.
?end

## 또각또각 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- clip-clop (sound of hard heels or hooves on a hard surface)
- click-clack of footsteps
### 예
- 하이힐 소리가 복도에서 또각또각 울렸다.
	- The sound of high heels clicked down the hallway.
- 말이 또각또각 길을 걸어갔다.
	- The horse clip-clopped along the road.
- 구두 소리가 또각또각 들렸어요
  - I heard the click-clack sound of high heels
- 또각또각 걸어오는 소리가 났어요
  - There was the sound of someone walking click-clack
?end
