## 말이에요 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- I'm saying / what I mean is (polite)
- it's about... (stating what one is talking about)
### 예
- 제 말이 그 말이에요.
	- That's exactly what I'm saying.
- 그게 아니라 이 말이에요.
	- Not that — I mean this.
- 중요한 말이에요.
	- It's an important thing to say.
### Notes
- Standard spelling is 말이에요 (말 ends in a consonant, so 이에요 — not 예요). 말이예요 and 말예요 are common misspellings/non-standard contractions of it
- 말이요 (separate card) is a different form, used when echoing what someone said: 제 말이요!
?end

## 말이요 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- as for (what was just said) (polite echo)
- exactly! (in 제 말이요)
### 예
- 제 말이요!
	- That's exactly what I'm saying!
- 그 말이요? 저도 그렇게 생각해요.
	- That remark? I think so too.
### Notes
- 말이요 = 말 + 이요. It echoes someone's words politely; for "it is what I mean" use 말이에요 (separate card)
- 말이예요 / 말예요 are non-standard — use 말이에요
?end
