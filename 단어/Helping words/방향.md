# 방향과 위치 (Direction & Position)

## 안 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- inside
- in
### 예
- 가방 안에 지갑이 있어요.
	- My wallet is inside the bag.
- 안에서 기다릴게요.
	- I'll wait inside.
### Notes
- Opposite: 밖 (outside). 안 is also the negation adverb ('not'), a different word
- Related: 안쪽 (inner part, see [[위치와_장소]])
<!--SR:!2027-04-18,386,295-->
?end

## 옆 #card #location #beside
?begin
### 뜻
- beside
- side
- next to

### 예
- 옆에 앉아요
  - Sit beside me
- 집 옆
  - Next to the house
- 은행 옆에 편의점이 있어요.
	- There's a convenience store next to the bank.
- 제 옆에 앉으세요.
	- Please sit next to me.
?end

## 앞 #card #location #front
?begin
### 뜻
- front
- ahead
### 예
- 앞에 있어요
  - It's in front
- 집 앞
  - In front of the house
- 학교 앞에서 만나요.
	- Let's meet in front of the school.
- 앞으로 열심히 공부할게요.
	- I'll study hard from now on.
### Notes
- 앞으로 can also mean 'from now on / in the future'

## 근처에 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- near
- around
### 예
- 우리 집 근처에 공원이 있어요.
	- There's a park near my house.
- 이 근처에 맛있는 식당이 있어요?
	- Is there a good restaurant around here?
### Notes
- 근처 = vicinity (a place nearby); 주변 = surroundings
<!--SR:!2027-08-27,513,270-->
?end

## 주변에 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- around
- near
### 예
- 학교 주변에 카페가 많아요.
	- There are many cafés around the school.
- 제 주변에는 한국어를 배우는 친구가 많아요.
	- Among the people around me, there are many friends learning Korean.
### Notes
- 주변 can also mean the people around you
<!--SR:!2026-12-13,268,230-->
?end

## 뒤 #card #location #behind
?begin
### 뜻
- behind
- back
- after (in time)
### 예
- 건물 뒤에 주차장이 있어요.
	- There's a parking lot behind the building.
- 회의가 끝난 뒤에 전화할게요.
	- I'll call after the meeting ends.
- 뒤에 있어요
  - It's behind
- 집 뒤
  - Behind the house
### Notes
- 뒤 can also mean 'after' in time

?end

## 밑 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- bottom
### 예
- 책상 밑에 고양이가 있어요.
	- The cat is under the desk.
- 나무 밑에서 쉬었어요.
	- I rested under the tree.
### Notes
- 밑 ≈ 아래 (below); 밑 often suggests directly underneath
?end

## 방면 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- direction
- side
- area
### 예
- 서울 방면 버스를 타세요.
	- Take the bus heading toward Seoul.
- 공항 방면으로 가는 길이 막혀요.
	- The road toward the airport is jammed.
### Notes
- 방면 ≈ 방향, but it is used with a place name (서울 방면) or a field (연구 방면)
?end

## 정면 #card
?begin

## 어디서 #card #location #from-where #at-where #location-questioning
?begin
### 뜻
- from where
- at where
### 예
- 어디서 만날까요?
	- Where shall we meet?
- 이 가방은 어디서 샀어요?
	- Where did you buy this bag?
### Notes
- 어디서 = 어디에서 (at where / from where)
?end

## 어디로 #card #location #to-where #toward-where #direction-questioning
?begin
### 뜻
- to where
- toward where
### 예
- 어디로 가요?
	- Where are you going?
- 이 길은 어디로 연결돼요?
	- Where does this road lead?
?end

## 어느 #card #location #which #what #questioning-modifier #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- which
- what
### 예
- 어느 나라 사람이에요?
	- Which country are you from?
- 어느 것이 더 맛있어요?
	- Which one tastes better?
### Notes
- 어느 + noun; 어느 날 = 'one day'
?end

## 지방 #card #location #province #region #local-area

- local area

?begin

- neighborhood
- district
