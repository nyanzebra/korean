# 부분 (Portion & Part)

## 중 일부 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- some among X
- few from X
### 예
- 저는 이제 여러분들 중 일부가 무엇을 생각하시는지 압니다.
	- So I know what some of you must be thinking right now.
- 이것들은 그 문제들 중 일부입니다. 그럼, 저는 어떻게 했을까요?
	- These are a few of them. So, what did I do?
- 그러한 체험을 가진 사람은 전 인구 중 일부에 국한되어 있다
	- Only a few in the whole population have had such experiences.
### Notes
- See also 중 (during, among) in 시간 개념.md

## 쪽수가 한참 모자라 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- we're badly outnumbered (slang)
- we're far short in numbers
### 예
- 상대편은 서른 명인데 우리는 열 명이라 쪽수가 한참 모자라.
	- They have thirty people and we have ten, so we're badly outnumbered.
- 쪽수가 한참 모자라서 싸워 봤자 질 거야.
	- We're so badly outnumbered that we'll lose if we fight.
### Notes
- 쪽수 is slang for headcount (its standard meaning is the page count); 한참 here means 'by far'
### Tags

## 티끌 모아 태산 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- specks of dust gathered become a great mountain
### 예
- 매일 천 원씩 저축하는 게 별거 아닌 것 같아도, 티끌 모아 태산이야
	- Saving 1,000 won every day might not seem like much, but little by little it all adds up
- 매일 조금씩 거짓말했는데, 티끌 모아 태산이라고 결국 큰 문제가 됐어.
	- I lied a little every day, and just like dust gathering into a mountain, it eventually became a huge problem
### Notes
- **티끌** — a speck of dust or tiny particle
- **모아** — gathered/collected (from 모으다)
- **태산** — Mount Tai (a great mountain); used to mean an enormous amount
- A 속담 (proverb); full form: 티끌 모아 태산이다
?end
