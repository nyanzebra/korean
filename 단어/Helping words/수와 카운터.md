# 수 (Numbers)

## 스무 #card #number #twenty #score #multiple
?begin
### 뜻
- twenty (native Korean)
### 예
- 스무 살이 되면 독립하고 싶어요.
	- When I turn twenty, I want to become independent.
- 사과 스무 개를 샀어요.
	- I bought twenty apples.
### Notes
- 스무 is used before a counter; on its own it is 스물
?end

## 하나하나 #card #number #one-by-one #each-and-every #individually
?begin
### 뜻
- one by one
- each and every
### 예
- 하나하나 천천히 설명해 주세요.
	- Please explain one by one, slowly.
- 아이의 행동 하나하나가 사랑스러워요.
	- Every single thing my child does is adorable.
?end

## 첫째 #card #number #first #primary #number-one
?begin
### 뜻
- first
- number one
### 예
- 저는 삼 형제 중 첫째예요.
	- I'm the eldest of three brothers.
- 첫째, 시간을 잘 지켜야 해요.
	- First, you must be punctual.
### Notes
- Ordinals: 첫째, 둘째, 셋째, 넷째, 다섯째 ... also used for birth order. 첫 번째 / 두 번째 are the 'number of times' forms
?end

## 채 #card #counter #buildings #houses #structures
?begin
### 뜻
- counter for buildings
### 예
- 마을에는 집이 열 채 있어요.
	- There are ten houses in the village.
- 한옥 두 채를 사서 게스트하우스로 만들었다.
	- He bought two hanok and turned them into a guesthouse.
### Notes
- Different word from 채 meaning 'as is / while' (옷을 입은 채로)
?end

## 마디 #card #counter #joints #sections #segments
?begin
### 뜻
- counter for words / utterances (한마디)
- counter for joints / sections
### 예
- 한마디 말도 없이 떠났어요.

?end

## 피트 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- feet (unit of length, ft ≈ 30.5 cm)

### 예
- 6피트 높이
  - 6 feet tall
- 이 비행기는 3만 피트 상공을 날고 있어요
  - This plane is flying at an altitude of 30,000 feet

### Notes
- Loanword from English "feet" (singular: foot); not to be confused with the name Pete
?end
