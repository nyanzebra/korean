## 자네 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- you (older to younger person - formal/polite)
- informal term for "you" from older speaker

### 예
- 자네한테 할 말이 있어
  - I have something to tell you
- 이건 자네랑 나만 알고 있어
  - Only you and I know this
- 자네가 좋아해?
  - Do you like it?

### Notes
- Related: 너 (you - informal), 당신 (you - formal), 그대 (you - old-fashioned)

?end
