# 미디어 (Media & Entertainment)

## 책 #card #media #book #publication #reading
?begin
### 뜻
- book
### 예
- 도서관에서 책을 빌렸어요
  - I borrowed a book from the library
- 이 책은 영화로도 만들어졌어요
  - This book was also made into a movie
### Notes
- 책을 읽다: to read a book
?end
