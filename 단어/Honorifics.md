## 잡수다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- eat (honorific)
- have (honorific)
### 예
- 아예 날 잡수시지!!!
  - Why don't you just eat me whole! (i.e. "Oh, kill me now!")
- 진지 잡수셨습니까
  - Have you had your meal?
- 왕이 잡수시는 것, 최고로 맛있는 요리
  - what the king eats, the most delicious dish (a dish fit for a king)
### Notes
- 잡수다 is a higher honorific form of 먹다; 드시다 is the more common everyday honorific
- 진지 = honorific word for 밥 (meal)
### Tags
#honorific #eating #formal #polite #royal
?end

## 주시다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to give (honorific of 주다)
- to kindly do (something for someone) when used as -아/어 주시다
### 예
- 선생님께서 저에게 책을 주셨어요
  - The teacher gave me a book
- 도와주셔서 감사합니다
  - Thank you for helping
- 와주셔서 정말 감사합니다
  - Thank you so much for coming
- 와주셔서 정말 기뻐요
  - I'm so glad you came
- 말씀해 주십시오
  - Please tell me (formal)
- 기다려 주시오
  - Please wait (formal, authoritative/old-fashioned)
- 물 좀 주실래요?
  - Could you give me some water?
- 주신 선물, 잘 쓸게요
  - I'll put the gift you gave me to good use
- 내일 도와주실 거예요?
  - Will you help (me) tomorrow?
### Notes
- 주셔서 = "for/because you gave (did)", used to thank someone: -아/어 주셔서 감사합니다
- 주신 = past modifier ("that you gave"); 주실 = future modifier ("that you will give")
- 주십시오 = formal polite request; 주시오 = formal/authoritative or old-fashioned request (하오체); 주실래요? = polite "would you mind giving/doing..."
- Related: 주다 (to give), 드리다 (to give to a superior), 받으시다 (to receive, honorific)

?end

## 자네 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- you (older to younger person - formal/polite)
- informal term for "you" from older speaker
### 예
- 자네한테 할 말이 있어
  - I have something to tell you
- 이건 자네랑 나만 알고 있어
  - Only you and I know this
- 자네가 좋아해?
  - Do you like it?
### Notes
- Related: 너 (you - informal), 당신 (you - formal), 그대 (you - old-fashioned)
?end
