# 가족 (Family)

## 가족 #card #family
?begin
### 뜻
- family
### 예
- 가족이 몇 명이에요?
  - How many people are in your family?
- 가족을 사랑해요
  - I love my family
- 우리 가족들은 모두 건강해요
  - All the members of our family are healthy
### Notes
- 가족들: family members (plural form)
- Related: 식구 (household member), 친척 (relative)
?end

## 부모님 #card #family #parents
?begin
### 뜻
- parents
### 예
- 부모님이 오셨어요
  - My parents came
- 부모님께 전화했어요
  - I called my parents
- 부모님께서 집에 계세요
  - My parents are at home
### Notes
- 부모: parents (plain form, e.g. 부모와 자녀 "parents and children"); 부모님 is the respectful form used for one's own or someone else's parents
- Related: 아버지, 어머니, 자녀
?end
