# 나이 (Age)

## 늙다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be old, to get old
- to age
### 예
- 우리 할아버지는 많이 늙으셨어요
  - My grandfather has gotten very old
- 늙기 전에 세계 여행을 하고 싶어요
  - I want to travel the world before I get old
### Notes
- Opposite: 젊다 (to be young)
- Related: 노화 (aging), 어른 (adult)
<!--SR:!2026-12-21,278,254-->
?end

## 젊다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be young
- to look young
### 예
- 어머니는 나이보다 젊어 보이세요
  - My mother looks younger than her age
- 젊을 때 많이 경험해 보세요
  - Try lots of experiences while you're young
### Notes
- Opposite: 늙다 (to be old)
<!--SR:!2028-02-04,571,256-->
?end

## 어른 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- adult
- elder, senior person
### 예
- 어른이 되면 혼자 살고 싶어요
  - When I become an adult, I want to live on my own
- 집안 어른들께 먼저 인사드려라
  - Greet the elders of the family first
### Notes
- 대인 (大人): "adult" as a ticket/fare category (대인 요금 = adult fare), also "a person of great character"
- Opposite: 아이 (child)
- Merged from 어른 and 대인 (SR schedule kept from 대인)
<!--SR:!2026-12-11,145,270-->
?end

## 노화 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- aging
### 예
- 나이가 들면 노화가 진행돼요
  - As you get older, aging progresses
- 규칙적인 운동은 노화를 늦춰 줘요
  - Regular exercise slows down aging
### Notes
- 노화하다: to age
- Related: 늙다 (to be old)
?end
