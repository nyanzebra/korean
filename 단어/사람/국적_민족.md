?begin

- foreigner
- foreign person
- alien

### 예
- 내국인과 외국인
  - natives and foreigners

  - There are many foreigners on the street

  - foreign registration card

## 내국인 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- native
- domestic person
- citizen

### 예
- 내국인과 외국인
  - natives and foreigners
- 대한민국 내국인
  - South Korean citizen
- 내국인 우대
  - preferential treatment for citizens

### Notes
- Related: 내국 (domestic), 외국인 (foreigner), 시민 (citizen)

?end
