## 부인 #card #family #wife-formal
?begin
### 뜻
- wife (polite word, mainly for someone else's wife)
- Mrs., madam (title for a married woman)
- to deny / to disown (verb 부인하다 — a different word with the same spelling)

### 예
- 사장님 부인께서 오셨어요
  - The boss's wife has come
- 부인과 함께 오세요
  - Please come with your wife
- 그는 그 사실을 부인했어요
  - He denied that fact
- 그 말은 부인할 수 없어요
  - That statement can't be denied

### Notes
- Different words: 부인 (夫人, wife) vs 부인하다 (否認, to deny) — same spelling, different meaning and Hanja
- 부인 is polite for another person's wife; for one's own wife use 아내 or 집사람 (see 가족과_관계.md)
- Related: 아내 (wife), 부정 (negation)

?end
