## 전원 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- power (supply), power source (電源)
- all members, everyone (全員)
- countryside, rural area (田園)
### 예
- 전원을 끊다
  - shut off power
- 전원이 달려들어 일했다
  - everyone dove in and worked together
- 선장을 비롯해서 승무원 전원이 사망했다
  - the whole crew, including the captain, died
- 나는 조용한 전원 풍경을 뇌리에 그렸다
  - I pictured the quiet pastoral scenery in my mind
### Notes
- Three different words with the same spelling; 전원을 켜다 / 끄다 means "to turn the power on / off"

?end

## 통화 #card #phone #call
?begin
### 뜻
- phone call
- to talk on the phone (통화하다)
- currency (通貨)
### 예
- 친구와 오랫동안 통화했어요
  - I talked with my friend on the phone for a long time
- 지금 통화 중이에요
  - The line is busy / I'm on a call right now
- 전화로 통화해요
  - I talk on the phone
### Notes
- 통화하다 (verb) is merged into this card
- 통화 meaning currency (通貨) is a different word with the same spelling
