# 스포츠 용품 (Sports Equipment)

## 방망이 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- bat (baseball)
- club, cudgel
### 예
- 선수가 방망이를 힘껏 휘둘렀어요
  - The player swung the bat with all his might
- 아이가 야구 방망이를 새로 샀어요
  - The child bought a new baseball bat
### Notes
- 야구 방망이 / 야구 배트: baseball bat
- 방망이 also means a club used for beating laundry or pounding rice cake
