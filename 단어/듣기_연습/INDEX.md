# 듣기_연습 (Listening Practice)

This folder contains listening comprehension materials and exercises for Korean language learners.

## Contents

### 듣기 Mooky the Parrot - Korean Listening Practice
A structured listening practice resource with various listening comprehension exercises.

### Riddle/Clue Materials (clue_1.md)
Guessing game format with progressive clues in Korean. Topics include:
- Common vegetables (onions, etc.)
- Mathematical/language-based riddles
- Geography (DMZ - Demilitarized Zone)
- Natural phenomena (Equator)
- Countries and landmarks
- Natural seasonings
- US cities

Each riddle provides:
1. Definition or category
2. Historical/scientific facts
3. Additional identifying information

### Skit Materials (skit_1.md)
Conversational practice from actual dialogue and skit scenarios, including:
- Outdated expressions and style criticism
- Performance and entertainment vocabulary
- Consumer perspectives and marketability
- Casual conversation vocabulary

**Example vocabulary covered**: 구닥다리 (old-fashioned), 의도하다 (intend), 성능 (performance), 상품성 (marketability), 입장 (perspective), 반응 (reaction)

---

## Purpose

These materials are designed to develop:
- **Listening comprehension** through varied formats
- **Vocabulary** in conversational contexts
- **Pronunciation awareness** through exposure to natural Korean
- **Situational understanding** through riddles and dialogue

**Difficulty Level**: Intermediate (conversational Korean)

These materials complement vocabulary study by providing authentic listening contexts.
