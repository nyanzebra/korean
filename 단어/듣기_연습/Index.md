This folder contains listening comprehension materials and exercises for Korean language learners.

## 📖 Files in this folder

- [[듣기_어휘.md|듣기_어휘]] - Vocabulary cards (`#card`) made from the Mooky and skit word lists below
- [[듣기 Mooky the Parrot_Korean Listening Practice_2024-03-02.md|Mooky the Parrot listening practice (2024년 3월 2일)]] - Raw transcript of expressions (vocabulary converted in 듣기_어휘)
- [[clue_1.md|Riddle/clue materials]] - guessing game with progressive clues in Korean (vegetables, DMZ, equator, seasonings, US cities...). Each riddle gives a definition, historical/scientific facts, then extra identifying information.
- [[skit_1.md|Skit materials]] - conversational practice from dialogue/skit scenarios (outdated expressions, performance, marketability, casual conversation). Vocabulary list converted in 듣기_어휘: 구닥다리 (old-fashioned), 의도하다 (intend), 성능 (performance), 상품성 (marketability), 입장 (perspective), 반응 (reaction)

## Purpose

- **Listening comprehension** through varied formats
- **Vocabulary** in conversational contexts
- **Pronunciation awareness** through exposure to natural Korean
- **Situational understanding** through riddles and dialogue

Difficulty: intermediate (conversational Korean)
