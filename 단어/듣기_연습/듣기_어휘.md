## 해가 길어졌다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- the days have gotten longer (literally: the sun has gotten long)

### 예
- 봄이 되니까 해가 길어졌다
  - Now that it's spring, the days have gotten longer
- 벌써 일곱 시인데 아직 환하네, 해가 길어졌다
  - It's already seven o'clock and still bright, the days have gotten longer

### Notes
- Opposite: 해가 짧아졌다 (the days have gotten shorter)
?end

## 현타 온다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- I'm having a reality check (slang; short for 현실 자각 타임)
- a sudden, deflating realization of reality

### 예
- 통장 잔액을 보니까 현타 온다
  - Looking at my bank balance, reality hits me
- 밤새 열심히 했는데 결과가 이렇다니 현타 온다
  - I worked hard all night and the result is like this, reality check

### Notes
- 현타 = 현실 자각 타임 (reality-awareness time)
- Slang: usually used when you suddenly see how disappointing reality is
?end

## 솔직한 대답을 원해? #card
?begin

### 예
- 솔직한 대답을 원해? 아니면 듣기 좋은 대답을 원해?
  - Do you want an honest answer? Or one that's nice to hear?
- 솔직한 대답을 원하면 말해 줄게
  - If you want an honest answer, I'll tell you

?end

## 한동안 잠잠했어 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- it was quiet for a while (it used to be loud or exciting, but not now)

### 예
- 한동안 잠잠했는데 요즘 또 시끄러워졌어요
  - It was quiet for a while, but lately it's gotten noisy again
- 소문이 한동안 잠잠했어요
  - The rumor died down for a while

### Notes
- 한동안: for a while (one word; the transcript wrote 한 동안)

?end

## 너네 여기 소풍 왔어? #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Did you come here for a picnic? (a scolding for not taking things seriously)

### 예
- 일은 안 하고 놀기만 하네, 너네 여기 소풍 왔어?
  - You're just playing and not working. Did you come here for a picnic?
- 연습 시간에 웃고 떠들기만 하면 어떡해, 너희 소풍 왔어?
  - What are you doing laughing and chatting during practice? Did you come for a picnic?

### Notes
- 너네 = 너희 (colloquial form); the transcript has 너네들
- 소풍: an outing, a picnic
?end

## 불문율 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- unwritten rule
- unspoken rule (不文律)

### 예
- 이 회사에는 막내가 먼저 출근하는 불문율이 있어요
  - There's an unwritten rule at this company that the youngest member arrives first
- 그건 우리 사이의 불문율이야
  - That's an unwritten rule between us

### Notes
- Spelling: 불문율, not 불문률 (after a vowel or ㄴ, the suffix is 율)
- Related: 보이지 않는 규칙 (unspoken rule) in [[../세상/환경_사회.md|환경_사회]]
?end

## 지름신 강림하셨어 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- the god of impulse buying has descended upon me
- I had a shopping splurge (slang)

### 예
- 세일 기간에 지름신이 강림하셔서 옷을 열 벌이나 샀어요
  - The god of impulse buying descended during the sale and I bought ten pieces of clothing
- 또 지름신 강림하셨어? 이번 달 카드값 어쩌려고?
  - The god of splurging has descended again? What will you do about this month's card bill?

### Notes
- 지르다: (slang) to splurge, to buy something impulsively; 지름신 = 지르다 + 신 (god)
- 강림하다: to descend (from the heavens)
- Related: 얼버무리다 (to mumble) in [[../표현_관용구/회화표현.md|회화표현]] (the Mooky transcript also has 얼버무리지 마)
?end

## 얘가 #card
?begin
### 뜻

### 예
- 얘가 제 동생이에요
  - This is my younger sibling
- 얘가 요즘 성능이 좀 떨어졌어요

## 벌어지다 vs 부러지다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- 벌어지다: to widen; to open up; to happen (an incident occurs)
- 부러지다: to break (a long, hard object snaps)

### 예
- 두 팀의 점수 차이가 벌어졌어요
  - The score gap between the two teams widened
- 운동장에서 큰 싸움이 벌어졌어요
  - A big fight broke out on the playground
- 나뭇가지가 바람에 부러졌어요
  - The branch snapped in the wind

### Notes
- The two verbs sound similar, so they are easy to confuse
?end

## 마침내 / 결국 / 급기야 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- 마침내: finally, at long last (after effort or waiting)
- 결국: in the end, eventually (any result)
- 급기야: in the end, finally (reaching an extreme or bad result)

### 예
- 마침내 시험에 합격했어요
  - I finally passed the exam
- 고민하다가 결국 포기했어요
  - After agonizing, in the end I gave up
- 몇 달을 무리하더니 급기야 병원에 실려 갔어요
  - After overworking himself for months, it finally went so far that he was taken to the hospital

### Notes
- 마침내 is usually positive; 결국 is neutral; 급기야 usually follows a worsening situation
?end
