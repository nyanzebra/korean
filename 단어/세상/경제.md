## 경기 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- game; match; competition (a sports sense)
- economic conditions; the state of the economy (경기, a different meaning)

### 예
- 경기는 한참 이따 시작해
  - The game will start quite a bit later
- 축구 경기
  - soccer match
- 경기 결과
  - game result
- 요즘 경기가 안 좋아서 장사가 안 돼요
  - The economy is bad these days, so business is slow

### Notes
- 경기 (競技): a sports match, game
- 경기 (景氣): the state of the economy (경기가 좋다/나쁘다)
- Related: 시합 (match), 경제 (economy)
?end

## 해고하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to dismiss; to fire
- to lay off

### 예
- 회사가 직원 열 명을 해고했어요
  - The company fired ten employees
- 부당하게 해고당했어요
  - I was dismissed unfairly

### Notes
- 해고시키다 is also heard (causative), but 해고하다 is the standard form
- 해고당하다: to be fired
- Related: 해고 (dismissal), 그만두다 (to quit), 퇴직 (retirement)
?end
