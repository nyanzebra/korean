# 법 (Law)

## 판결 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- court judgment
- decision
- ruling
- verdict

### 예
- 법원에서 아직 판결을 내리지 않았어
  - The court hasn't made a judgment yet
- 판사는 정당방위였다고 판결했어
  - The judge ruled it was self-defense
- 유죄 판결, 무죄 판결
  - guilty verdict, not guilty verdict
- 판결문
  - written judgment

### Notes
- Related: 판사 (judge), 판단 (judgment), 법원 (court)
?end

## 정당방위 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- self-defense
- legitimate (justifiable) defense

### 예
- 판사는 정당방위였다고 판결했어
  - The judge ruled it was self-defense
- 정당방위 주장
  - claim of self-defense
- 정당방위는 법적으로 인정돼요
  - Self-defense is legally recognized

### Notes
- Spelling: the legal term is 정당방위 (正當防衛), not 정상방위
- Related: 방위 (defense), 법 (law)
?end

## 죄 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- crime
- sin
- guilt
- offense

### 예
- 죄를 짓다
  - to commit a crime/sin
- 무슨 죄를 지었어요?
  - What crime did you commit?
- 죄책감
  - guilt, sense of guilt
- 중죄
  - serious crime

### Notes
- Related: 범죄 (crime), 죄인 (criminal, sinner), 유죄 (guilty)
?end

## 불법 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- illegal
- unlawful
- illegality

### 예
- 불법 행위를 하지 마세요
  - Don't engage in illegal activities
- 그것은 불법이에요
  - That is illegal
- 불법 시위
  - illegal protest/demonstration

### Notes
- Opposite: 합법 (legal), 적법 (lawful)
- Related: 법 (law), 위법 (violation of the law)
?end
