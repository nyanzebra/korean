# 사고ㆍ안전 (Accidents & Safety)

**Overview:** Vocabulary related to accidents, disasters, safety measures, and public order — useful for news articles and everyday safety topics.

---

## 대책 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- a countermeasure

### 예
- 정부는 화재 예방을 위한 대책을 마련했어요
  - The government prepared countermeasures to prevent fires
- 근본적인 대책이 필요해요
  - A fundamental countermeasure is needed

?end

---

## 대피하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to evacuate
- to take shelter

### 예
- 지진이 나자 사람들이 운동장으로 대피했어요
  - When the earthquake hit, people evacuated to the playground
- 화재 경보가 울리면 즉시 대피하세요
  - If the fire alarm goes off, evacuate immediately

?end
