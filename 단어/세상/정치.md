## 탄핵 / 탄핵되다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- impeachment; removal from office
- 탄핵되다: to be impeached

### 예
- 탄핵 절차
  - impeachment procedure
- 대통령 탄핵
  - presidential impeachment
- 이것으로 트럼프는 두 번 탄핵된 유일한 미국 대통령이다
  - With this, Trump is the only U.S. president to have been impeached twice
- 탄핵 결정에 반대하는 촛불 시위는 불법이라는 정부의 발표가 있자 검찰은 조치를 취했다
  - The prosecution took action after the government announced that the candlelight vigils opposing the impeachment decision were illegal

### Notes
- Legal process to remove an official from office
- Verb: 탄핵하다 (to impeach)
?end
