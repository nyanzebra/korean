## 절약하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to save; to economize
- to be frugal; to conserve
- to be economical

### 예
- 돈을 절약해야 해요
  - We need to save money
- 시간을 절약하다
  - to save time
- 여비를 절약하다
  - to save travel expenses
- 에너지를 절약하다
  - to conserve energy
- 우리가 아무리 돈을 절약할지라도 집을 살 만큼의 돈은 마련하지 못할 것이다
  - Even if we save every penny, we won't be able to raise enough money to buy a house

### Notes
- Related: 저축하다 (to save money in the bank), 낭비하다 (to waste), 검소하다 (to be frugal)
- Noun: 절약 (saving, frugality)
- From a quote: "To choose time is to save time."
?end
