# 정도와 강조 (Degree & Emphasis)

## 꽤 #card #adverb #quite #fairly #considerably #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- quite
- fairly
- rather
- considerably

### 예
- 꽤 좋아요
  - It's quite good
- 꽤 많은 사람들
  - quite a lot of people
- 꽤 크다
  - fairly big
- 꽤 비싸요
  - It's quite expensive

### Notes
- Related: 상당히 (considerably), 꽤나 (quite a bit), 제법 (quite/fairly)
?end

## 조금만 / 좀만 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- just a little
- just a bit
- wait a moment (in 좀만 기다려요)

### 예
- 좀만 기다려요
  - Wait just a moment
- 좀만 더 작으면 좋겠어요
  - I wish it were just a little smaller
- 조금만 도와주세요
  - Please help me just a little

### Notes
- 좀만 is the colloquial contraction of 조금만
- Related: 조금 (a little), 잠깐 (a moment)
?end

## 참으로 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- truly
- really
- indeed
- genuinely

### 예
- 참으로 좋습니다
  - That is truly good / It is really wonderful
- 참으로 멋있어요
  - That is truly amazing

### Notes
- Related: 정말 (really), 진짜 (really), 참 (truly), 진정으로 (genuinely)
?end

## 전혀 #card #adverb #not-at-all #never #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- at all
- not at all
- never (with negative)
- entirely
- completely

### 예
- 전혀 모르겠어요
  - I don't understand at all
- 전혀 몰라요
  - I don't know at all
- 전혀 괜찮아요
  - It's completely fine
- 전혀 다르다
  - It's entirely different

### Notes
- Related: 절대 (absolutely), 조금도 (not at all), 아무 (no/any)
?end

## 뿐이다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- only, just
- nothing but

### 예
- 농담일 뿐입니다
  - It's just a joke.
- 시작일 뿐입니다
  - It's only the beginning.

### Notes
- 뿐이다 = only, nothing but (Noun + 뿐이다 / Verb-ㄹ 뿐이다); 뿐입니다 is the formal polite form
?end
