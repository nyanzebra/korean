## 꽤 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- quite
- fairly
- rather
- considerably

### 예
- 꽤 좋아요
  - It's quite good
- 꽤 많은 사람들
  - quite a lot of people
- 꽤 크다
  - fairly big

### Notes
- Related: 상당히 (considerably), 꽤나 (quite a bit), 제법 (quite/fairly)

?end

## 좀만 #card

### 예
- 좀만 기다려요

- 좀만 작아요
  - It's just a little small

### Notes
- Related: 조금 (a little), 조금만 (just a little), 잠깐 (a moment)

?end
