?begin
### 뜻
- I'm sorry (formal)
### 예
- 늦어서 죄송합니다
	- I'm sorry for being late
- 실수해서 정말 죄송합니다
	- I'm truly sorry for my mistake

	- I'm sorry for the trouble
?end

	- Sorry I'm late

?begin
### 뜻
