## 속상해하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be upset
- to be distressed
- to be troubled

### 예
- 속상해하지 마세요
  - Don't be upset
- 그 일 때문에 속상해했어요
  - I was upset because of that

### Notes
- Related: 화나다 (get angry), 슬프다 (be sad), 괴로워하다 (be troubled)
?end

## 쪼그라들다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to shrink; to shrivel up
- to become smaller (and wrinkled)

### 예
- 풍선에 바람이 빠져서 쪼그라들었어요
  - The balloon lost its air and shriveled up
- 햇볕에 말린 대추가 쪼그라들었어요
  - The dates dried in the sun shriveled up
- 자신감이 쪼그라들어서 발표를 못 했어요
  - My confidence shrank, so I couldn't give the presentation

### Notes
- Related: 줄어들다 (to decrease), 작아지다 (to become smaller), 오그라들다 (to shrivel/curl up)
?end

## 제정신이니? #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Are you in your right mind?
- Are you out of your mind?
- Is this sensible?

### 예
- 제정신이니? 이 시간에 전화를 하면 어떡해
  - Are you out of your mind? How could you call at this hour?
- 이게 제정신이야? 그 돈으로 이런 걸 샀다고?
  - Is this sensible? You bought this with that money?

### Notes
- Idiomatic expression questioning someone's sanity/judgment
- Related: 미쳤다 (be crazy), 정신 (mind/sense)
?end

## 파김치가 되다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be completely exhausted
- to be worn out (literally "to become green-onion kimchi")

### 예
- 하루 종일 일했더니 파김치가 됐어요
  - After working all day, I'm completely worn out
- 파김치가 되어서 집에 오자마자 쓰러졌어요
  - I was so exhausted that I collapsed as soon as I got home

### Notes
- Idiomatic: like green-onion kimchi (파김치) that has gone limp and soft, i.e. completely worn out
- Related: 지치다 (to be tired out), 탈진 (exhaustion)
?end

## 한두 가지가 아니다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- there are more than just one or two (i.e. very many)
- countless

### 예
- 우리 회사의 문제는 한두 가지가 아니에요
  - Our company's problems are not just one or two
- 이사하면서 불편한 점이 한두 가지가 아니었어요
  - The inconveniences of moving were countless

### Notes
- Idiomatic expression: 한두 (one or two) is written as one word
- Related: 수없이 많다 (to be countless)
?end

## 사는 게 힘들다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- life is hard
- living is tough
### 예
- 요즘은 사는 게 너무 힘들어요.
  - Life is so hard these days.
- 사는 게 힘들어도 힘내세요.
  - Even if life is hard, hang in there.
### Notes
- 사는 게 = 사는 것이 (contracted); here 사는 것 means 인생 (life)
- Related: 인생이 힘들다
?end

## 조심하세요 (말 / 운전 / 감기) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- be careful (with ~)
- watch out for ~
### 예
- 말조심하세요.
  - Watch what you say.
- 운전 조심하세요.
  - Drive safely.
- 환절기에는 감기 조심하세요.
  - Be careful not to catch a cold during the change of seasons.
### Notes
- N + 조심하세요: used as a warning or a farewell wish
- Related: 환절기 (change of seasons, see [[날씨]])
?end
