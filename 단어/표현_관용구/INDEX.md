### 4. **인사말.md** (Greetings & Polite Expressions) - ~193 lines

- 얼른 나으세요 (get well soon)

- 축하합니다 (Congratulations)
- 건배 (Cheers/Toast)
- And 20+ additional greetings

### 5. **일상표현.md** (Daily Expressions) - ~134 lines
Common everyday phrases:
- 생각보다 어렵지 않네요 (it's not as difficult as I thought)
- 언제쯤 도착 예정이세요? (what's your ETA?)
- 저랑 음악 취향이 비슷하시네요 (we have similar music tastes)
- 나가기 귀찮아요 (I'm too lazy to go out)
- Grammar patterns and expressions

### 6. **사과_감사.md** (Apologies & Gratitude) - ~151 lines
Expressions for apologizing and thanking:
- **Apologies:** 죄송합니다, 미안해요, 정말 미안해, 잘못했어요, 용서해 줄래요?
- **Gratitude:** 고마워요, 감사합니다, 정말 고맙습니다, 도움을 줘서 고마워, 신경써줘서 고맙습니다
- **Responses:** 천만에요, 괜찮아요
- Formal and casual levels

### 7. **도움_제안.md** (Asking & Offering Help) - ~129 lines

- 괜찮으면 도와드릴게요 (If you don't mind, I'll help)
- And offering support phrases

## Organization Strategy

Files are organized by:
1. **Semantic category** (what the phrases are about)
2. **Function** (what they're used for)
3. **Context** (formal/casual, specific situations)

Each file uses the flashcard format with `#card` markers for spaced repetition learning in Obsidian.

---

## Usage Guide

- **Finding a phrase?** Check the semantic category (opinion, apologies, tech, etc.)
- **Spaced repetition?** Files are formatted for Obsidian flashcard plugin
- **Grammar patterns?** Look for `~` markers indicating grammatical structures
- **Formal vs. casual?** Each file indicates politeness levels
