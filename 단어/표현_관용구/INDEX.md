# 표현_관용구 (Expressions & Idioms) - Index

This folder contains Korean expressions, idioms, proverbs, and situation-specific phrases organized by category.

## Files in this Category

### 1. **인사말.md** (Greetings & Polite Expressions)
- Greeting phrases (오랜만이네요, etc.)
- Thanks and apologies (감사합니다, 죄송합니다, etc.)
- Welcome and congratulations
- Toasts and well-wishes
- ~326 lines

### 2. **일상표현.md** (Daily Expressions)
- Common responses and reactions
- Gratitude and apologies (duplicated from common usage)
- Philosophical quotes on:
  - Satisfaction (만족)
  - Productivity (생산성)
  - Making others happy (타인을 행복하게)
- ~203 lines

### 3. **속담_및_격언.md** (Proverbs & Sayings)
- Korean proverbs and traditional sayings
- Chinese idioms (사자성어)
- Philosophical quotes on:
  - Mistakes (실수)
  - Regret (후회)
  - Control (통제)
  - Bad convictions (나쁜 신념)
- ~257 lines

### 4. **상황별표현.md** (Situation-Specific Phrases)

- ~93 lines
