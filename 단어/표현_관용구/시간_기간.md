## 어서 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- right away
- hurry up
- quickly

### 예
- 어서 와요
  - Come right in / Come here quickly
- 어서 시작합시다
  - Let's start right away

### Notes
- Related: 빨리 (quickly), 얼른 (hurry), 서둘러 (hasten)

?end

## 한참 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- for quite a while
- a long time
- quite a bit

### 예
- 한참 기다렸어요
  - I waited for quite a while
- 한참을 걸었어요
  - I walked for a long time

### Notes
- Related: 오래 (long), 한동안 (for a while), 오랫동안 (for a long time)

?end
