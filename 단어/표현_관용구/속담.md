# 속담 (Korean Proverbs)

Traditional Korean proverbs and wise sayings about life, relationships, and wisdom.

## 친구 따라 강남 간다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to go along with a friend, even somewhere one had not planned to go
- to follow others' lead (peer pressure)

### 예
- 친구 따라 강남 간다고, 동생이 친구 따라 학원에 다니기 시작했어요
  - Following a friend to Gangnam, my younger sibling started going to an academy just because a friend goes
- 나는 가고 싶지 않았는데 친구 따라 강남 간다고 따라갔어요
  - I didn't want to go, but I tagged along because a friend was going

### Notes
- Literally: "Following a friend, you go to Gangnam"

## 시작이 반이다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- starting is half finishing

### 예
- 시작이 반이라고 하니까 일단 시작해 봐요
  - They say starting is half the battle, so let's just start
- 가장 힘든 부분이 시작하는 거예요
  - The hardest part is getting started
- 막상 시작하면 그렇게 어렵지 않아요
  - Once you actually start, it's not that difficult

### Notes
- Literally: "The beginning is half"; the standard form of the proverb is 시작이 반이다

?end

## 지피지기 백전백승 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- If you know your enemy and yourself, you will win every battle (知彼知己 百戰百勝)

### 예
- 당신의 식습관을 제대로 알면 다이어트는 백전백승이에요
  - If you know your eating habits well, you will master your diet
- 지피지기 백전백승이라고, 상대 팀부터 분석해 봅시다
  - Know your enemy and yourself, they say, so let's start by analyzing the other team

### Notes
- Originates from Sun Tzu's The Art of War

## 그 아버지에 그 아들 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Like father, like son (literally "that father, that son")
- The apple doesn't fall far from the tree

### 예
- 아들도 아버지처럼 운동을 잘해요, 그 아버지에 그 아들이에요
  - The son is good at sports just like his father, like father like son
- 성격도 똑같아요, 정말 그 아버지에 그 아들이네요
  - Their personalities are exactly the same, truly like father like son
- 당신은 어머니와 붕어빵이에요
  - You are the spitting image of your mother

### Notes
- Similar expressions: 아버지와 판박이예요 (he is a carbon copy of his father), 어머니와 붕어빵이에요 (she looks just like her mother)
- 당신은 아버지/어머니를 꼭 닮았어요: You really take after your father/mother
?end

## 발 없는 말이 천 리 간다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Words without feet travel a thousand miles (rumors spread easily)

### 예
- 발 없는 말이 천 리 간다더니, 어제 한 얘기가 벌써 온 회사에 퍼졌어요
  - Words without feet travel a thousand miles; what I said yesterday has already spread across the whole company
- 말조심하세요, 발 없는 말이 천 리 가요
  - Watch what you say, words travel far

### Notes
- Related: 낮말은 새가 듣고 밤말은 쥐가 듣는다 (the birds hear what is said by day and the mice by night)
?end

## 종로에서 뺨 맞고 한강에서 화풀이한다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Getting slapped on Jongno and venting one's anger at Hangang (taking one's anger out on an innocent party)

### 예
- 회사에서 혼나고 와서 동생한테 화풀이하다니, 종로에서 뺨 맞고 한강에서 화풀이한다더니
  - He got scolded at work and took it out on his younger sibling, a classic case of being slapped on Jongno and venting at Hangang
- 시험을 망쳤다고 나한테 짜증 내지 마, 종로에서 뺨 맞고 한강에서 화풀이하는 거야?
  - Don't take your bad exam out on me. Are you being slapped on Jongno and venting at Hangang?

### Notes
- The more standard form is 종로에서 뺨 맞고 한강에서 눈 흘긴다
?end

## 길고 짧은 것은 대 봐야 안다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- You won't know which is long or short until you measure them (there's only one way to find out)

### 예
- 길고 짧은 것은 대 봐야 안다고, 누가 더 잘하는지는 직접 겨뤄 봐야 알아요
  - You have to measure to know which is longer, so we can only know who is better by competing
- 결과는 해 봐야 알아요, 길고 짧은 것은 대 봐야 알죠
  - You'll only know the result by trying; you have to measure to know which is longer
?end

## 소 잃고 외양간 고친다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Fixing the barn after losing the cow (closing the stable door after the horse has bolted; too little, too late)

### 예
- 소 잃고 외양간 고친다더니, 해킹을 당한 뒤에야 보안을 강화했어요
  - Closing the barn door after the cow is gone, they only strengthened security after being hacked
- 시험이 끝난 다음에 공부하는 건 소 잃고 외양간 고치는 격이에요
  - Studying after the exam is over is a case of fixing the barn after losing the cow

### Notes
- 잃다 = to lose (not 앓다, which means to be ill)
?end

## 모기 보고 칼 빼기 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Seeing a mosquito and drawing a knife (Overreacting to small problems)

### 예
- 벌레 한 마리에 소리를 지르다니, 모기 보고 칼 빼기네요
  - Screaming over a single bug is like drawing a sword at a mosquito
- 작은 실수에 그렇게 화낼 필요 없어요, 모기 보고 칼 빼기예요

## 빈 수레가 요란하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- An empty wagon makes the most noise (Those with little to show make the most noise)

### 예
- 빈 수레가 요란하다더니, 제일 시끄럽게 떠드는 사람이 제일 아는 게 없어요
  - An empty wagon makes the most noise; the person who talks the loudest knows the least
- 실력이 없는 사람일수록 말이 많아요, 빈 수레가 요란한 법이죠
  - The less skilled a person is, the more they talk; an empty wagon makes the most noise

### Notes
- 허장성세 (虛張聲勢): bluffing or putting on a show of strength without substance
?end

## 호랑이 굴에 가야 호랑이 새끼를 잡는다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Nothing ventured, nothing gained (you must enter the tiger's den to catch its cub)
- 호랑이 굴에 들어가다: to walk into danger, to take a great risk

### 예
- 호랑이 굴에 가야 호랑이 새끼를 잡는다고, 위험해도 도전해 봐야죠
  - Nothing ventured, nothing gained, so you have to take the risk and try
- 이건 호랑이 굴에 들어가는 거예요
  - This is like walking into the tiger's den

### Notes
- Idiomatic: 호랑이 굴에 들어가다 on its own means to put oneself in danger
?end

## 고래 싸움에 새우 등 터진다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- When whales fight, the shrimp's back breaks (when the powerful clash, the weak get hurt)

### 예
- 두 회사가 가격 전쟁을 벌이는 바람에 하청 업체만 피해를 봤어요, 고래 싸움에 새우 등 터진다더니
  - Because of the price war between the two companies, only the subcontractors suffered, a case of whales fighting and the shrimp's back breaking
- 부모님이 싸우시면 아이들만 힘들어요, 고래 싸움에 새우 등 터지는 거죠
  - When parents fight, only the kids have a hard time

### Notes
- Collateral damage: the weak bystander gets hurt
- Related vocab: 고래 (whale), 새우 (shrimp), 터지다 (to burst), 방관자 (bystander)
?end
