# 사자성어 (Chinese Four-Character Idioms)

Chinese four-character idioms commonly used in Korean.

---

## 작심삼일 (작심삼일) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- New Year's resolution; giving up after 3 days
- Inability to maintain determination or commitment

### 예
- 매년 새해에 작심삼일로 끝나요
  - My New Year's resolutions end after three days every year
- 헬스장을 끊었지만 작심삼일이 될까봐 걱정이에요
  - I got a gym membership but I'm worried it will end after three days

### 유래 (Origin)
- 작심 (作心) = to determine, to resolve
- 삼일 (三日) = three days
- This idiom reflects the human tendency to lose motivation quickly

## 새옹지마 (塞翁之馬) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- "a blessing in disguise"; fortune and misfortune are unpredictable and intertwined
- what seems like bad luck may turn out well, and vice versa

### 예
- 인생은 새옹지마예요
  - Life is like saeongjima (you never know if something is a blessing or a curse)
- 그 일이 새옹지마가 될 줄 누가 알았겠어요
  - Who would have known that would turn out to be a blessing in disguise

### 유래 (Origin)
- 새옹 (塞翁) = an old man at the frontier/border, 지 (之) = possessive particle, 마 (馬) = horse
- From a story where an old man's horse ran away (seemingly bad), then returned with another horse (seemingly good), and so on — illustrating that fortune and misfortune are hard to judge in the moment

?end
