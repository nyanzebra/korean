# 사자성어 (Chinese Four-Character Idioms)

Chinese four-character idioms commonly used in Korean.

---

## 작심삼일 (작심삼일) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- New Year's resolution; giving up after 3 days
- Inability to maintain determination or commitment

### 예
- 매년 새해에 작심삼일로 끝나요
  - My New Year's resolutions end after three days every year
- 헬스장을 끊었지만 작심삼일이 될까봐 걱정이에요
  - I got a gym membership but I'm worried it will end after three days

### 유래 (Origin)
- 작심 (作心) = to determine, to resolve
- 삼일 (三日) = three days
- This idiom reflects the human tendency to lose motivation quickly
