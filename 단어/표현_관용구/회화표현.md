## 얼버무리다 #card #verb #speaking
?begin
### 뜻
- to mumble; to slur one's words
- to speak ambiguously, to be evasive
- to fudge (an answer)

### 예
- 말 얼버무리지 마
  - Don't mumble, speak clearly
- 질문을 받자 그는 대답을 얼버무렸어요
  - When he was asked, he gave a vague answer
- 그는 질문에 대답하지 않고 얼버무렸어요
  - He evaded the question and mumbled something
- 질문을 받자 그는 대답을 얼버무렸다.
  - When asked the question, he fudged his answer.

### Notes
- Also heard in the Mooky the Parrot listening practice (얼버무리지 마)
- Related: 우물쭈물하다 (to hesitate)
?end
