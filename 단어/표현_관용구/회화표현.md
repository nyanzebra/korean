# 회화 표현 (Conversational Expressions)

## 기본 표현

### 얼버무리다
- Definition: slur/speak ambiguously, mumble
- Example: 말 얼버무리지 마 - speak clearly

### 까먹다
- Definition: forget
- Example: 이따 하려고 하면 까먹어 - if you want to do later you might forget

### 너 좋을 대로 해
- Definition: do as you like
- Synonym: 네 하고 싶은 대로 해

### 둘러보다
- Definition: look around
- Example: 둘러 볼고 올게요 - come and look around

### 말이 되다
- Definition: makes sense
- Example: 이게 말이 된다고 생각해 - do you think this makes sense?

### 눈 높이를 좀 낮춰
- Definition: lower your standards (literally: lower the height of your eyes)
- Related: 눈높이 - (the height of one's eyes/perspective)

### 아무 말 대잔치야
- Definition: full of random words
- Components: 대잔치 - big party

### 저게 어떻게 가능해
- Definition: how can that be possible

### 자동
- Definition: automatic

### 네 도움이 필요해
- Definition: need your help

### 이 거 내가 했다고 생각해
- Definition: do you think I did this?

### 나 어렸을데는 그딴거 없었어
- Definition: When I was young, there was no such thing
- Components: 
  - 그딴거 - such things (derogatory/dismissive)
  - 그런거 - similar things (neutral)

### 딴건 몰라도 이건 꼭 봐야돼
- Definition: Even though I don't know about other things, you must watch this

### 이거 진짜 재미있어
- Definition: This is really fun

## 표현과 감정

### 있을 노레 어디서 들어 본 거 같애
- Definition: I think I heard this song somewhere before

### 제 이름 어떻게 아세요
- Definition: How do you know my name?

### 시간 내 주셔서 감습니다
- Definition: Thank you for making time

### 산이랑 바다 중에 뭐가 좋아
- Definition: Between mountains and the sea, which do you prefer?

### 내가 어디까지 얘기했지
- Definition: Where was I (when speaking about stuff)?

### 나도 모르게 웃음이 튀어나왔어
- Definition: I burst out laughing without realizing
- Components: 튀어나오다 - pop out

## 식품 및 물품

### 유통기한 살짝 지났어
- Definition: The expiration date has slightly passed
- Components:
  - 유통 - circulation of product
  - 기한 - deadline
  - 살짝 - a little bit, slightly

### 꼼꼼히 확인해
- Definition: Check meticulously

### 오타 있는지 꼼꼼히 확인해
- Definition: Check meticulously for typos

### 기반, 근본, 기본
- Definition: foundation, original, basic

### 환풍기 vs 선풍기
- 환풍기 - air ventilation fan (AC)
- 선풍기 - regular fan (portable)
- Context: 선풍기 바람 글루 가 - can we have the wind from fan go this way?

## 문장 구조 표현

### 이게 말이 된다고 생각해
- Definition: Do you think this makes sense?
- Related expression: 말이 되다 - makes sense

### 그 사람 좋을 대로 해
- Definition: Do as they like

### 아무튼 / 암튼
- Definition: Anyway, in any case
