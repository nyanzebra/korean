## 평가하다 #card #evaluation #assessment #judgment #academic #business
?begin
### 뜻
- to evaluate; to assess
- to appraise; to rate

### 예
- 학생의 성과를 평가했어요
  - I evaluated the student's performance
- 공정하게 평가해 주세요
  - Please evaluate it fairly

### Notes
- Related: 평가 (evaluation), 평가받다 (to be evaluated), 판단하다 (to judge)
?end

## 방지하다 #card #prevention #protection #safety #process
?begin
### 뜻
- to prevent; to avert
- to protect against

### 예
- 사고를 방지하기 위해 안전 교육을 해요
  - We give safety training to prevent accidents
- 감염 확산을 방지해야 해요
  - We must prevent the spread of infection

### Notes
- Related: 방지 (prevention), 예방하다 (to prevent, esp. illness), 막다 (to block)
?end

## 매기다 #card #rating #scoring #evaluation #process
?begin
### 뜻
- to assign (a grade, rank, price)
- to rate; to score

### 예
- 상품에 가격을 매겼어요
  - I put a price on the product
- 선수들의 순위를 매기다
  - to rank the players

### Notes
- Related: 점수를 매기다 (to grade/score), 순위 (rank), 평가하다 (to evaluate)
?end
