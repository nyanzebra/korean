# 완료와 완성 (Completion & Perfection)

## 완료 (完了) #card #completion #process #administrative
?begin
### 뜻
- completion (of a task or process)
- done; processed; concluded

### 예
- 결제가 완료되었습니다
  - Your payment has been completed
- 다운로드가 완료될 때까지 기다려 주세요
  - Please wait until the download is complete
- 신청 완료
  - Application complete

### Notes
- Focuses on a task or process being finished; often formal, administrative or technical
- Compare: 완성 (a creation is finished), 완치 (full recovery from illness)
- 완료: _doing_ something is finished / 완성: _making_ something is finished

?end

## 완성 (完成) #card #completion #creation
?begin
### 뜻
- completion (of something made or built)
- finished; perfected; fully realized

### 예
- 드디어 소설을 완성했어요
  - I finally finished the novel
- 작품이 완성되었어요
  - The work is complete
- 집이 거의 완성됐어요
  - The house is almost finished

### Notes
- Focuses on something being fully made or built, reaching its final form
- Compare: 완료 (a task or process is done)

?end

## 완벽 (完璧) #card #perfection #quality
?begin
### 뜻
- perfection
- flawless; perfect

### 예
- 완벽한 사람은 없어요
  - No one is perfect
- 그는 완벽주의자예요
  - He is a perfectionist
- 발음이 완벽해요
  - Your pronunciation is perfect

### Notes
- No flaws, no room for improvement; often used for quality or personality
- Related: 완벽하다 (to be perfect), 완벽주의 (perfectionism)

?end

## 완전 (完全) #card #completeness #adverb
?begin
### 뜻
- complete; total; perfect
- completely; totally (casual adverb use)

### 예
- 이 음식 완전 맛있어요!
  - This food is so delicious!
- 완전한 자유를 원해요
  - I want complete freedom
- 완전 다른 얘기예요
  - That's a totally different story

### Notes
- Used as an adverb in casual speech: 완전 = "totally / so ..."
- Related: 완전히 (completely, 수와_양.md), 완전하다 (to be complete)
- Compare: 완벽 (flawless)

?end

## 완치 (完治) #card #recovery #medical
?begin
### 뜻
- full recovery (from an illness)
- complete cure

### 예
- 완치됐어요
  - I have fully recovered
- 이 병은 완치가 어려워요
  - This disease is hard to cure completely

### Notes
- Used specifically for recovering completely from illness
- Related: 치료 (treatment), 회복 (recovery), 낫다 (to get better)

?end

---

### Quick comparison

| Word | Core idea | Typical context |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| 완료 | task is done | admin, tech, processes |
| 완성 | creation is done | art, writing, building |
| 완벽 | no flaws | quality, personality |
| 완전 | totally, fully | adverb, emphasis |
| 완치 | fully healed | medical |
