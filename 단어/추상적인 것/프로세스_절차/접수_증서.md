# 접수와 증서 (Reception, Acceptance & Certificates)

## 접수 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- receive
- accept
- receipt
- take over

### 예
- 그 건물은 군용으로 접수되었다
  - The building was requisitioned for army use.
- 원서를 접수하다
  - to receive [accept] an application
- 담당관은 서류의 접수를 거부했다
  - The official in charge refused to accept the papers.
- 주문을 접수하다
  - take an order.
- 영어가 전세계를 접수하고 있다. 아니, 그런 것 같다.
  - English is taking over the world, or so it seems.

### Tags
#reception #acceptance #taking-over #administrative #formal
?end

## 상장 #card #certificate #award #business #stock-market
?begin
### 뜻
- listing (on a stock exchange) (上場)
- certificate of merit; award certificate (賞狀)
- mourning ribbon (喪章)

### 예
- 그는 개근[입선] 상장을 받았다
  - He was awarded a certificate for perfect attendance[winning a prize].
- 그 회사는 작년에 주식 시장에 상장했어요
  - That company went public on the stock market last year

### Notes
- 상장 (上場) listing / 상장 (賞狀) award certificate / 상장 (喪章) mourning ribbon: homonyms
- Related: 상장하다 (to list), 상 (prize), 증서 (certificate)
?end
