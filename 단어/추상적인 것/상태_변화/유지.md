# 유지와 연속 (Maintenance, Continuation & Succession)

?begin
### 뜻
- maintain
- sustain
- continue

### 예
- 하지만 한국과 다르게 북한은 중국과 소련의 도움 없이는 전쟁을 지속할 수 없었다.
  - However, unlike South Korea, North Korea could not continue their war without the help of China and the Soviet Union.

  - The stakes are just too high to continue the way we are going.

- Compare: 유지하다 – keep some state as it is

## 유지하다 #card #maintenance #preserve #sustain #process
?begin
### 뜻
- maintain
- preserve
- keep

### 예
- 원자재 가격이 국제 시장에서 약세를 유지하고 있다
  - Raw material prices remain in a bearish trend in the international market.
- 이 집을 유지하는데는 무척 많은 경비가 든다
  - It costs a lot to maintain[keep up] this house.

### Notes
- sustain some state (keep it as it is)
- Compare: 지속하다 – keep doing some action
- https://hinative.com/questions/19360784
?end

## 첩출하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- arise in succession
- occur frequently

### 예
- 불행한 일이 첩출하다
  - have one misfortune after another/have a series of misfortune

### Notes
- Formal/literary
- Related: 연속 (succession), 잇따르다 (to occur one after another), 빈번하다 (to be frequent)

#succession #frequent #continuous #occurring #series #formal
?end
