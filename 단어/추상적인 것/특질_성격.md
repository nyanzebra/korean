# 특질과 성격 (Characteristics and Personality)

## 고집 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- stubbornness
- obstinacy
- willfulness
- determination

### 예
- 고집이 세다
  - to be stubborn
- 그 아이는 고집이 있어요
  - That child is stubborn
- 고집을 부리다
  - to be stubborn
- 나쁜 고집
  - bad stubbornness

### Notes
- Related: 고집불통 (stubborn person), 완고하다 (stubborn), 의지 (willpower)

?end

## 고집불통 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- stubborn person
- pigheaded person
- someone who won't listen
- obstinate person

### 예
- 두 고집불통들 중간에 껴서 피곤하겠다
  - It must be tiring to be caught between two stubborn people
- 그 사람은 진짜 고집불통이야
  - That person is really stubborn
- 고집불통처럼 행동하다
  - to act like a stubborn person

### Notes
- Related: 고집 (stubbornness), 완고한 사람 (obstinate person), 말을 안 듣다 (won't listen)

?end

## 의도 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- intention
- intent
- purpose
- aim

### 예
- 의도가 너무 뻔해
  - Your intention is too obvious
- 그럴 의도가 전혀 없었어요
  - I had no intention of doing that
- 의도를 했든 안 했든
  - Whether intentional or not
- 좋은 의도
  - good intention

### Notes
- Related: 목적 (purpose), 계획 (plan), 뜻 (intention/meaning)

?end
