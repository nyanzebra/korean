# 특질과 성격 (Characteristics and Personality)

## 고집 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- stubbornness
- obstinacy
- willfulness
- determination

### 예
- 고집이 세다
  - to be stubborn
- 그 아이는 고집이 있어요
  - That child is stubborn
- 고집을 부리다
  - to be stubborn
- 나쁜 고집
  - bad stubbornness

### Notes
- Related: 고집불통 (stubborn person), 완고하다 (stubborn), 의지 (willpower)
?end

## 고집불통 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- stubborn person
- pigheaded person
- someone who won't listen
- obstinate person

### 예
- 두 고집불통들 중간에 껴서 피곤하겠다
  - It must be tiring to be caught between two stubborn people
- 그 사람은 진짜 고집불통이야
  - That person is really stubborn
- 고집불통처럼 행동하다
  - to act like a stubborn person

### Notes
- Related: 고집 (stubbornness), 완고한 사람 (obstinate person), 말을 안 듣다 (won't listen)
?end

## 유능하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be competent
- to be capable
- to be able

### 예
- 저 분은 아주 유능한 검사야
  - That person is a very competent prosecutor
- 유능한 직원
  - A capable employee

### Notes
- Related: 능력 (ability), 역량 (competency), 재능 (talent)
?end
