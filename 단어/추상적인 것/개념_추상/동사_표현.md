# 동사와 표현 (Verbs & Expressions)

## 만만치 않다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to not be easy to deal with
- to be no pushover

### 예
- 이번 시험이 만만치 않을 것 같아요
  - This exam doesn't seem like it'll be easy
- 그 상대는 만만치 않아요
  - That opponent isn't someone to underestimate

### Notes
- Related: 쉽지 않다 (to not be easy), 호락호락하지 않다 (to not be a pushover)
?end
