## 끊임없다 #card #vocabulary
?begin
### 뜻
- to be continuous, unceasing
### 예
- 끊임없는 노력
	- Continuous effort
- 끊임없이 나아가다
	- To proceed continuously
?end

## 저지르다 #card #vocabulary
?begin
### 뜻
- to commit (a crime/mistake), to make
### 예
- 실수를 저지르다
	- To make a mistake
- 범죄를 저지르다
	- To commit a crime
?end

## 낙담하다 #card #vocabulary
?begin
### 뜻
- to become discouraged, to lose heart
### 예
- 실패해서 낙담하다
	- To become discouraged after failure
- 낙담하지 말다
	- Don't lose heart
?end

---

### Note
From: "살면서 저지를 수 있는 가장 큰 실수는 실수할까 봐 끊임없이 걱정하는 것이다"
(In life, the biggest mistake you can make is to constantly worry about making mistakes.)
