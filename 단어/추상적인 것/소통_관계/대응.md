# 대응 (Response & Treatment)

## 대하다 #card #treatment #response #interaction #regarding #toward #multiple-meanings
?begin
### 뜻
- about
- against
- toward
- through
- to treat (a person)
- to deal with (a person)
- to face (someone/something)

### 예
- 우연히 무언가를 발명하거나 발견한 사람들에 대한 재미있는 이야기가 많이 있다.
  - There are many funny stories about people who invented or discovered things accidentally.
- 러시아에 대한 각종 제재와 제한으로 러시아 선수들도 고통을 받고 있다.
  - With various sanctions and restrictions being used against Russia, its athletes are also suffering.
- 마지막으로, 인터넷에서 사람들에게 친절하게 대하세요.
  - Finally, be kind to people on the internet.
- 사람을 대하기 싫어하다
  - don't like to treat/receive people
- 그는 손님을 늘 친절하게 대해요
  - He always treats customers kindly
- 부모님을 공손하게 대해야 해요
  - You should treat your parents respectfully

### Notes
- ~에 대해(서) = about, regarding; ~에 대한 + noun = "about/toward ..."
- ~을/를 대하다 = to deal with, to treat (people)
- Used for people and attitudes; for handling things, see (을/를) 취급하다
- Also cited as (을/를) 대하다
?end

## 취급하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to treat (as in "to handle / to regard as")
- to handle (things)

### 예
- 이 물건은 깨지기 쉬우니까 조심해서 취급해 주세요
  - This item is fragile, so please handle it with care
- 그는 저를 어린아이처럼 취급해요
  - He treats me like a child

### Notes
- Used mainly for things (goods, materials) or for regarding someone/something "as" something; for people's behavior toward others, see (을/를) 대하다
- Related: 다루다 (to handle, deal with)
?end
