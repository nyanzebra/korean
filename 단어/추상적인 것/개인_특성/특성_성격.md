# 특성 & 성격 (Traits & Personality)

## 특질 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- trait, characteristic, quality (more formal / written)

### 예
- 그는 지도자로서의 특질을 갖추고 있어요.
	- He has the qualities of a leader.
- 이 식물은 독특한 특질을 가지고 있어요.
	- This plant has a unique characteristic.

### Notes
- 특질: inherent nature, fundamental characteristic (more formal)
- 특성: unique trait, distinctive feature (see 개념_추상/개념.md)
- 특징: notable feature, distinguishing mark (see 개념_추상/개념.md)
- 특기: special skill, forte (next card)
?end

### Notes

## 변덕스럽다 #card #trait #fickle
?begin
### 뜻
- to be fickle
- to be changeable
- to be capricious
- to have a personality that changes often
### 예
- 변덕스럽다 = 성격이 자주 바뀌다
	- fickle = one's personality changes often
- 그 사람은 변덕스러워요
	- That person is fickle
- 변덕스러운 날씨
	- Changeable weather
- 기분이 변덕스럽다
  - to have a fickle/changeable mood
- 성격이 변덕스럽다
  - to have a capricious personality
- 날씨가 변덕스럽다
  - the weather is unpredictable/changeable
- 기쁘다가 슬프다가 기분이 변덕스러웠어요
  - My mood changed fickly between happy and sad

### Notes
- 변덕 (noun): fickleness, whim; 변덕을 부리다 = to be capricious
- Related: 기분파 (moody person), 일관성 (consistency)
?end

## 자존심 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- pride
- ego
- self-respect
- dignity

### 예
- 자존심이 있는 사람
	- a self-respecting person.
- 하지만, 결국 관계는 자존심보다 더 중요하게 여겨져야 합니다.
	- But, ultimately, relationships should be valued more than pride.
- 자존심을 지키다
  - To maintain one's pride
- 자존심이 상하다
  - To have one's pride hurt

### Notes
- 자존심 is often about "not wanting to lose face"; 자존 is more formal "self-respect"; 자부심 is pride in one's achievements
- See also 자존, 자부심, 자부하다; 자랑스럽다 (감정_심리/감정.md)

## 곱다 #card #character #beauty #delicate #soft #elegant
?begin
### 뜻
- fine
- wonderful
- beautiful
- soft/delicate
- numb
- suffer a loss
### 예
- 마음씨가 곱다
	- noble-minded
- 허리가 곱다
	- have a bent back
- 살성이 곱다
	- delicate texture
- 그 처녀는 인물은 별로 없지만 그 대신에 마음씨가 고왔다
	- She wasn't very good-looking, but she had a very tender heart to make up for it.
- 곱게 다루다
	- kid's gloves, handle carefully/softly
- 고운 목소리
	- sweet voice
- 곱게 피어 있다
	- beautiful bloom

### Notes
- 곱다 has several homonyms: "fine/pretty/soft" (고운 목소리), and "numb/stiff" (손이 곱다)
?end

## 자부심 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- pride
- self-esteem
### 예

## 자부하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to take pride in (oneself)
- to be confident (of)

### 예
- 그녀는 자신이 아름답다고 자부하고 있었다
	- She had a very high opinion of her own beauty.
- 자부하지만 자만하지는 않습니다
	- I am self-confident, but not conceited.

### Notes
- 자부심 (noun): pride
