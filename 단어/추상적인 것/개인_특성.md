## 부분 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- part
- portion
- section

### 예
- 부분이 살짝 벌어졌어
  - A part has slightly opened/widened
- 전체의 일부분
  - A portion of the whole

### Notes
- Related: 부분적 (partial), 일부 (part/portion), 조각 (piece)

?end

## 유능하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be competent
- to be capable
- to be able

### 예
- 저 분은 아주 유능한 검사야
  - That person is a very competent prosecutor
- 유능한 직원
  - A capable employee

### Notes
- Related: 능력 (ability), 역량 (competency), 재능 (talent)

?end
