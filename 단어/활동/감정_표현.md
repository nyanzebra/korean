# 감정 표현 (Emotional Expression)

## 빠져들다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to fall into
- to get absorbed in
- to get lost in
- to fall for
- to become engrossed in
### 예
- 깊이 빠져드는 것 같아요
  - I seem to be falling deeply into it
- 그 영화에 빠져들었어요
  - I got absorbed in that movie
- 게임에 빠져드는 학생들
  - Students who get absorbed in games
- 그 소설에 완전히 빠져들었어요
  - I became completely absorbed in that novel
- 음악에 빠져들어서 시간 가는 줄 몰랐어요
  - I was so absorbed in the music that I lost track of time
### Notes
- Spelling: the dictionary form is 빠져들다; 빠져드는, 빠져드니 are its conjugations (ㄹ drops before ㄴ), so 빠져드다 is only a mistaken back-formation
- Related: 빠지다 (to fall into), 몰입하다 (to immerse), 집중하다 (to concentrate; see [[생각과 행동.md|생각과 행동]])
?end
