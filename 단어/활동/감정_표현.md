# 감정 표현 (Emotional Expression)

## 빠져드다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to fall into
- to get absorbed in
- to fall for
- to become engrossed in

### 예
- 깊이 빠져드는 것 같아요
  - I seem to be falling deeply into it
- 그 영화에 빠져들었어요
  - I got absorbed in that movie
- 게임에 빠져드는 학생들
  - Students who get absorbed in games

### Notes
- Related: 빠지다 (to fall into), 몰입하다 (to immerse), 집중하다 (to concentrate)

?end
