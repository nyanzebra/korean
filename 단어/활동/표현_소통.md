# 표현 (Expression)

## 표현 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- expression
- to express
- conveying ideas or feelings

### 예
- 의견을 자유롭게 표현해요
  - I express my opinion freely
- 얼굴 표현이 정말 좋아요
  - Your facial expression is really good
- 자신의 생각을 표현하는 것이 중요해요
  - It's important to express your own thoughts

### Notes
- Related: 표현하다 (to express), 소통 (communication), 의견 (opinion)

?end
