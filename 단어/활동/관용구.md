## 물 건너가다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be off the table; to have fallen through (lit. to have crossed the water)
### 예
- 그럼, 저녁 식사는 물 건너갔겠네요?
  - So I guess dinner is off the table, then?
- 폭우 때문에 소풍은 물 건너갔어요
  - The picnic is off because of the heavy rain
### Notes
- Used when a plan or hope has become impossible to achieve
?end

## 있는 힘을 다하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- use all of one's strength
- do one's utmost

### 예
- 있는 힘을 다해 바위를 밀었어요
  - I pushed the boulder with all my strength
- 그는 있는 힘을 다해 달렸어요
  - He ran with all his might

### Notes
- Related: 있는 힘껏 (with all one's might), 최선을 다하다 (do one's best)
?end

## 한눈 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- a glance
- a moment of distraction
- one eye

### 예
- 한눈에 반하다
  - fall in love at first sight
- 한눈을 팔다
  - be distracted / look away
- 한눈에 알아보다
  - recognize at a glance
- 운전 중에 한눈을 팔면 안 된다
  - You shouldn't be distracted while driving

### Notes
- 한눈에 = at a glance; 한눈(을) 팔다 = to take one's eyes off what one should be focusing on
?end
