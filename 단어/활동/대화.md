# 대화 (Conversation & Dialogue)

This file contains vocabulary specifically related to conversation, discussion, and dialogue interactions.

## 대화하다 #card #verb #converse #chat #dialogue
?begin
### 뜻
- to converse
- to have a conversation
### 예
- 한국어로 대화할 수 있어요.	I can converse in Korean.
- 선생님과 대화했어요.	I had a conversation with the teacher.
?end

?begin

## 말을 걸다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to speak to someone
- to start a conversation
- to talk to
### 예
- 나한테 말을 걸지 마	Don't talk to me
- 낯선 사람이 말을 걸었어요	A stranger spoke to me
- 먼저 말을 거는 게 어려워요	It's difficult to start a conversation first
### Notes
- Related: 대화하다 (converse), 말하다 (speak), 인사하다 (greet)
?end

## 대답하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to answer
- to respond
- to reply
### 예
- 질문에 대답해야 해요	I need to answer the question
- 빨리 대답했어요	I answered quickly
### Notes
- Related: 반응하다 (react), 응하다 (respond), 맞장구치다 (chime in)
?end

## 맞장구치다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to respond appropriately
- to chime in
- to react (to conversation)
- to play along
### 예
- 맞장구치다	to respond/react appropriately to someone's words
- 친구가 말할 때 맞장구를 쳐줬어요	I responded/reacted when my friend spoke
- 좋은 맞장구는 대화를 즐겁게 만들어요	A good response makes conversation enjoyable
### Notes
- Related: 반응하다 (react), 대답하다 (answer), 응하다 (respond)
?end

## 반응하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to react
- to respond
- to answer
### 예
- 반응하다	to react/respond
- 이 제안에 긍정적으로 반응했어요	We responded positively to this proposal
- 빠르게 반응해야 해요	We need to respond quickly
### Notes
- Related: 맞장구치다 (chime in), 대답하다 (answer), 응하다 (respond)
?end

## 오해하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to misunderstand
- to misinterpret
- to get wrong
### 예
- 오해하다 = 잘못 생각하다	to misunderstand / to think wrongly
- 저를 오해하지 마세요	Don't misunderstand me
- 오해했어요	I misunderstood
### Notes
- Related: 오해 (misunderstanding), 실수 (mistake), 착각 (confusion)
?end
