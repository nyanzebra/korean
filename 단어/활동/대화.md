# 대화 (Conversation & Dialogue)

Vocabulary for the *actions of conversation*: talking, asking, answering, discussing, reacting, and getting your point across. For expressing feelings and for relationships between people, see [[소통.md|소통]].

## 대화하다 #card #verb #converse #chat #dialogue
?begin
### 뜻
- to converse
- to have a conversation
### 예
- 한국어로 대화할 수 있어요
  - I can converse in Korean
- 선생님과 대화했어요
  - I had a conversation with the teacher
### Notes
- Related: 이야기하다 (talk), 말을 걸다 (start talking to someone), 소통 (communication)
?end

## 맞장구치다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to respond appropriately
- to chime in
- to react (to conversation)
- to play along
### 예
- 친구가 말할 때 맞장구를 쳐 줬어요
  - I chimed in to show I was listening when my friend spoke
- 좋은 맞장구는 대화를 즐겁게 만들어요
  - Good back-channel responses make a conversation enjoyable
- 그는 내 말에 맞장구치며 고개를 끄덕였어요
  - He nodded, agreeing with what I said
### Notes
- The same expression can be written 맞장구치다 (one word) or 맞장구를 치다 (noun + verb); both are accepted
- Related: 반응하다 (react), 대답하다 (answer), 응하다 (respond)
?end

## 지적 #card
