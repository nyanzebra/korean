## 적히다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be written
- to be put down
### 예
- 이름이 칠판에 적혀 있어요
  - The name is written on the blackboard
- 메모지에 적힌 전화번호를 봤어요
  - I looked at the phone number written on the memo
### Notes
- Passive form of 적다
?end

## 적다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to write (down)
- to note
- (adjective) to be few / little / small / low (a different word with the same form)
### 예
- 이 나라에서 80세 이상 되는 사람은 극히 적다
  - In this country, people over eighty years old are rare (few and far between)
- 가계부를 적다
  - keep a record of household expenses
- 수첩에 전화번호를 적었어요
  - I wrote the phone number in my notebook
- 그는 적은 임금으로 겨우 살아간다
  - He barely gets by on a small wage
?end
