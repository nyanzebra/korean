

## 성장하다 #card #verb #grow #develop #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- to grow
- to develop
- to mature
### 예
- 아이가 빨리 성장해요
  - The child is growing quickly
- 회사가 성장했어요
  - The company grew
- 경험을 통해 성장하다
  - to grow through experience
- 경제가 빠르게 성장하고 있어요
  - The economy is growing quickly
### Notes
- Related: 자라다 (grow up; see [[기본 동사.md|기본 동사]]), 발전하다 (develop), 성숙하다 (mature)
?end
