# 소통 (Communication & Relationships)

Vocabulary for expressing feelings and for relationships between people: praise, thanks, encouragement, closeness and distance, sharing. For the mechanics of conversation (asking, answering, discussing, reacting), see [[대화.md|대화]].

## 표현하다 #card #verb #express #convey #show
?begin
### 뜻
- to express
- to convey
### 예
- 감정을 표현하기 어려워요
  - It's hard to express emotions
- 의견을 자유롭게 표현하세요
  - Express your opinion freely
### Notes
- Related: 표현 (expression; see [[표현_소통.md|표현_소통]])
?end

## 언어 소통 #card
?begin
### 뜻

### 예

- 말은 안 통했지만 눈빛으로 서로 알 수 있었어요

### Notes
- Related: 소통하다 (to communicate), 의사소통 (communication), 얘기가 안 통하다 (see [[대화.md|대화]])
?end

## 꾸중을 듣다 #card

### 예
