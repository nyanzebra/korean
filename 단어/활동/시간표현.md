## 앞두다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be upcoming
- to be ahead
- to be imminent
### 예
- 중요한 시험을 앞두고 있어요
  - An important exam is coming up
- 졸업을 앞두다
  - graduation is imminent
- 새해를 앞두다
  - the new year is ahead
### Notes
- Related: 다가오다 (approach), 예정되다 (be scheduled), 앞서다 (lead)
?end

## 예정 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- plan, schedule
- be scheduled / be planned (예정이다 / -ㄹ 예정이다)

### 예
- 내일 출발할 예정입니다
  - It's scheduled to depart tomorrow.
- 회의가 3시에 시작할 예정입니다
  - The meeting is scheduled to start at 3 o'clock.

### Notes
- Verb-ㄹ 예정이다 = be scheduled to / plan to; 예정입니다 = formal polite form
- Related: 예정일 (due date) in 건강/의료.md
?end
