## 승진 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- promotion
- advancement

### 예
- 승진에 대한 내 희망
  - My hope for promotion
- 승진했어요
  - I got promoted
- 승진 기회
  - promotion opportunity

### Notes
- Related: 진급 (advancement), 승진하다 (get promoted), 직위 (position)

?end

## 입대 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to enter military service
- military enlistment

### 예
- 군대에 입대했어요
  - I entered military service
- 입대 일자
  - military service start date
- 이번 달에 입대해요
  - I'm enlisting this month

### Notes
- Related: 군대 (military), 전역 (discharge), 복무 (service)

?end
