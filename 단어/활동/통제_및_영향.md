## 끼치다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- influence
- impact
- cause
- affect
### 예
- 해를 끼치다
  - to cause harm
- 폐를 끼치다
  - to inconvenience (cause a bother)
- 소름이 끼치다
  - to get goose bumps
- 남에게 폐를 끼치는 일을 해서는 안 된다
  - You should not do anything that will be a nuisance to others
- 그의 유령 이야기를 듣고 우리 모두는 소름이 끼쳤다
  - His ghost story sent a chill through us all (made our blood run cold)
- 그것은 전통적 미풍양속에 해를 끼칠 우려가 있다
  - It is feared that it will have a harmful effect upon our long-established and fine customs
### Notes
- Related: 영향을 끼치다 (to have an influence on), 영향을 미치다 (same meaning)
?end

## 제압하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- overpower
- face down
- gain control
### 예
- 우리는 적의 기선을 제압하려고 그 항구를 공격했다
  - We attacked the harbor to gain the upper hand over the enemy
- 바다를 제압하다
  - gain control of the seas
### Notes
- 제압 (制壓) is control or mastery or supremacy
?end

## 접근하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to approach
- to get nearer
- to gain access to
### 예
- 서서히 목표에 접근하고 있어요
  - I'm gradually getting closer to my goal
- 그 지역에는 일반인이 접근할 수 없어요
  - Ordinary people cannot access that area
### Notes
- Spelling: this card was previously written 점근하다, which is not a standard verb. 접근하다 is the standard word for "to approach"
- 점근 (漸近, "gradual approach") is only used in math, e.g. 점근선 (asymptote)
- Related: 다가가다 (to approach; see [[움직이다.md|움직이다]])
?end
