## 마무리하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to finish
- to finalize
- to settle
- to wrap up
### 예
- 어서 마무리하자
  - Let's hurry and finish
- 일을 마무리했어요
  - I finished the work
- 회의를 마무리하다
  - to wrap up a meeting
### Notes
- Related: 끝내다 (finish), 완료하다 (complete), 정리하다 (settle), 마무리 (noun: finish, wrap-up; see [[기본 동사.md|기본 동사]])
?end

## 향하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to head toward
- to face
- to direct toward
### 예
- 그는 군사를 이끌어 서쪽으로 향했다
  - He led the army heading west
- 도시를 향해 가고 있어
  - I'm heading toward the city
- 그 방향을 향하다
  - to face that direction
### Notes
- Related: 방향 (direction), 향 (direction/scent), 나아가다 (advance)
?end

## 끊다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to cut
- to end
- to disconnect
- to sever
### 예
- 연결을 끊었어
  - I disconnected the connection
- 끊어, 이따 또 통화하자
  - Hang up. Let's call again later
- 연락을 끊다
  - to cut off contact
- 거래를 끊다
  - to end a deal/trade
- 담배를 끊다
  - to quit smoking
- 신문을 끊다
  - to cancel a newspaper subscription
### Notes
- Related: 자르다 (cut), 끝내다 (end), 분리하다 (separate)
?end

## 넘어가다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to cross over
- to go beyond
- to move on (to next topic)
- to transition
### 예
- 언덕을 넘어갔어
  - I went over the hill
- 다음 챕터로 넘어갔어
  - I moved on to the next chapter
- 중세에서 근대로 넘어갔어
  - (We) transitioned from medieval to modern times
- 그냥 넘어가자
  - Let's just move on / skip it
- 연기에 넘어갔어
  - I was fooled by the acting
### Notes
- Related: 넘다 (cross), 지나다 (pass), 변하다 (change)
?end

## 짓 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- deed
- act
- behavior
- activity (often negative)
### 예
- 저런 위험한 짓 따라 하지 마
  - Don't copy such dangerous behavior
- 무슨 짓을 한 거야?
  - What did you do?
- 네가 한 짓을 봐
  - Look at what you did
- 그건 미친 짓이야
  - That's crazy/insane behavior
- 네 아버지랑 하는 짓이 똑같아
  - You do the same things as your father
### Notes
- 짓 ≈ 일 (deed, work) / 행동 (action), but it usually carries a negative nuance
- Also appears in compounds: 몸짓 (gesture), 손짓 (hand gesture), 눈짓 (a glance/signal with the eyes)
- Related: 행동 (action), 버릇 (habit)
?end

## 서두르다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to hurry
- to rush
- to hasten
### 예
- 시간이 없어서 서둘러야 해요
  - I have to hurry because there's no time
- 서둘러 주세요
  - Please hurry
- 늦을 것 같아서 서둘렀어요
  - I thought I'd be late, so I hurried
### Notes
- 르-irregular: 서두르다 → 서둘러요 / 서둘렀어요 (not 서두러요, 서둘러다)
- Related: 빨리 (quickly), 어서 (right away), 재빨리 (swiftly)
?end
