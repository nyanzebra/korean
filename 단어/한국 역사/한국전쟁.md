?begin
### 뜻
- reunification (e.g. of North and South Korea)
- unification

### 예

  - Many people hope for the reunification of North and South Korea
- 통일이 되면 자유롭게 오갈 수 있을 거예요
  - Once reunification happens, people will be able to travel freely

  - North-South unification

### Notes
- Related: 민족 분단 (national division), 독립 (independence)
