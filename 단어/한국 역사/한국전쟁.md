## 휴전협정 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- a truce/ceasefire agreement

### 예
- 1953년에 휴전협정이 체결되었어요
  - The armistice agreement was signed in 1953
- 휴전협정 이후에도 긴장이 계속됐어요
  - Tensions continued even after the ceasefire agreement

### Notes
- Related: 포로 (prisoner of war), 전쟁 (war)

?end
