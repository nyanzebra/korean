?begin

?begin

- garbage bin

## 음쓰를 줄이다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to reduce waste
- to reduce garbage/trash
- to cut down on waste
### 예
- 음쓰를 줄이자
  - let's reduce food waste
- 남은 음식을 먹어서 음쓰를 줄여요
  - We reduce food waste by eating leftovers
### Notes
- 음쓰 is a colloquial abbreviation of 음식물 쓰레기 (food waste)
- Part of environmental conservation efforts
?end
