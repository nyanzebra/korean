## 혼나다 #card #scolding #punishment #trouble #social
?begin
### 뜻
- get frightened out of one's wits
- bitter experiences
- pay dearly
- get scolded
### 예
- 그 때문에 혼났다
	- I paid dearly for it./It cost me dear.
- 회의중에 낙서를 했다고 상사로부터 혼난 사람들도 있죠.
	- And they have bosses who scold them for doodling in the boardroom.
- 이제 혼나야 돼
	- You are in so much trouble.
- 갑자기 개가 튀어나와서 혼났어요
	- A dog suddenly jumped out and I got a real scare
?end

## 맞장구를 치다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to agree with what someone says
- to chime in in agreement
### 예
- 친구가 하는 말에 맞장구를 쳤어요
  - I chimed in to agree with what my friend said
- 그는 상대의 이야기에 맞장구를 잘 쳐요
  - He's good at agreeing with and responding to what others say
?end

## 꾸짖다 #card #scolding #discipline #reprimand #formal #social
?begin
### 뜻
- discipline
- scold
- reprimand
### 예
- 그는 아들의 나약[소심]함을 꾸짖었다
	- He scolded his son for his faintheartedness[cowardliness].
- 경찰관이 내게 고함을 질러 꾸짖었다
	- I was yelled at by a policeman.
- 엄마가 동생을 꾸짖을 때마다 순희는 그를 변호했다
	- Whenever Mom scolded her younger brother, Sunhee defended him.
?end

## 미팅 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- group blind date (esp. among college students)
- meeting
### 예
- 미팅을 하다
	- to go on a group blind date
- 친구가 소개해 준 미팅에서 지금의 남자친구를 만났어요
	- I met my current boyfriend at a group blind date my friend set up
### Notes
- In business contexts 미팅 simply means "meeting" (회의)
?end
