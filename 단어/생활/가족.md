## 효도하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be filial; to show filial piety to one's parents
### 예
- 부모님께 효도하고 싶어요
  - I want to be a good, dutiful child to my parents
- 취직해서 첫 월급으로 부모님께 효도했어요
  - I got a job and treated my parents with my first paycheck as an act of filial piety
### Notes
- Noun: 효도 (filial piety); related: 효자 (dutiful son), 불효 (unfilial behavior)
?end
