# 도구_기구 (Tools & Equipment)

## 확성기 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- megaphone
- loudspeaker
### 예
- 확성기로 말하다
  - to speak through a megaphone
- 확성기의 소리가 크다
  - the sound of the megaphone is loud
### Notes
- Related: 마이크 (microphone), 스피커 (speaker), 음성 (voice)
?end

## 못 #card #object #nail #spike #fastener
?begin
### 뜻
- nail
### 예
- 벽에 못을 박았어요
  - I drove a nail into the wall
- 못에 옷이 걸려서 찢어졌어요
  - My clothes snagged on a nail and tore
### Notes
- Homonym: 못 also means "cannot" (못 가요) or "a pond" (연못)
?end

## 드라이버 #card #object #screwdriver #tool #driver
?begin
### 뜻
- screwdriver
### 예
- 드라이버로 나사를 풀었어요
  - I loosened the screw with a screwdriver
- 드라이버가 어디 있어요?
  - Where is the screwdriver?
### Notes
- Also 드라이버 can mean "driver" (golf club, or a person who drives) — context decides
?end
