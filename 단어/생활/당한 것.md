## 도둑맞다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to have something stolen
- to be stolen from
- to get robbed
### 예
- 지갑을 도둑맞았어요
  - My wallet was stolen
- 차를 도둑맞은 사람들이 많아요
  - Many people have had their cars stolen
- 도둑맞을 뻔했다
  - I almost got robbed
### Notes
- Standard spelling is 도둑맞다 (one word, no space)
- Related: 도둑 (thief), 도둑질하다 (to steal), 소매치기당하다 (be pickpocketed)
?end


## 들치기하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- shoplift
### 예
- 들치기하다가 잡히다
	- caught shoplifting
- 가게에서 들치기하던 학생이 경찰에 붙잡혔어요
	- A student who was shoplifting at the store was caught by the police
?end


## 소매치기당하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- be pickpocketed
### 예
- 지하철에서 소매치기당했어요
  - I got pickpocketed on the subway
- 시장에서 소매치기를 당해서 지갑을 잃어버렸어요
  - I was pickpocketed at the market and lost my wallet
### Notes
- Also written 소매치기를 당하다
- Related: 도둑맞다 (to be stolen from)

?end
