# 물건_설명 (Describing Objects)

## 모양 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- shape
- form
- appearance
### 예
- 모양이 예뻐요
  - The shape is pretty
- 그게 무슨 모양이에요?
  - What shape is that?
- 하트 모양 쿠키
  - heart-shaped cookie
### Notes
- Related: 형태 (form), 크기 (size), 색깔 (color)
?end

## 형태 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- form
- shape
- structure
### 예
- 형태가 다양해요
  - There are various forms/shapes
- 어떤 형태인가요?
  - What form is it?
- 물은 고체, 액체, 기체의 세 가지 형태로 존재해요
  - Water exists in three forms: solid, liquid, and gas
### Notes
- More formal than 모양 (the two are close in meaning)
- Related: 모양 (shape), 구조 (structure), 형식 (format)
?end

## 감촉 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- texture
- feel
- tactile sensation
### 예
- 감촉이 부드러워요
  - The texture is soft
- 천의 감촉이 좋다
  - The fabric has a nice feel
### Notes
- Close in meaning to 촉감
- Related: 촉감 (texture), 부드럽다 (soft), 거칠다 (rough)
?end

## 촉감 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- texture
- feel
- touch sensation
### 예
- 촉감이 좋아요
  - The texture/feel is nice
- 목재의 촉감
  - The feel of wood
### Notes
- Close in meaning to 감촉
- Related: 감촉 (texture), 만지다 (touch), 부드럽다 (soft)
?end

## 재료 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- material
- ingredient
- raw material
### 예
- 이 옷의 재료가 뭐예요?
  - What's the material of this clothing?
- 좋은 재료로 만들었어요
  - It's made with good materials
- 요리 재료를 사러 시장에 갔어요
  - I went to the market to buy cooking ingredients
### Notes
- Used for: ingredients (cooking), materials (objects/clothing)
- Related: 재질 (material), 소재 (material), 성분 (component)
?end

## 재질 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- material (of an object)
- texture/composition of material
### 예
- 이건 무슨 재질이에요?
  - What material is this?
- 가죽 재질
  - leather material
### Notes
- More commonly used for materials of objects than 재료
- Related: 재료 (material/ingredient), 소재 (material), 성질 (property)
?end

## 네모나다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be square-shaped
- to be rectangular
- to be boxy
### 예
- 상자가 네모나요
  - The box is square/rectangular
- 이 책상은 네모난 모양이에요
  - This desk has a square/rectangular shape
### Notes
- 네모 = square/rectangle shape
- Related: 동그랗다 (round), 삼각형 (triangle), 모양 (shape)
?end

## 덩치가 크다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be bulky
- to be large in size
- to be big in build
### 예
- 덩치가 커요
  - It's bulky/large
- 그 사람은 덩치가 크다
  - That person is big in build
- 짐은 덩치가 크지만 가벼워요
  - The luggage is bulky but light
### Notes
- 덩치 = size/bulk (especially of objects or people's build)
- Related: 크다 (be big), 무겁다 (heavy)
?end
