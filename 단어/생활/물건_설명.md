# 물건_설명 (Describing Objects)

## 모양 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- shape
- form
- appearance
### 예
- 모양이 예뻐요
  - The shape is pretty
- 그게 무슨 모양이에요?
  - What shape is that?
- 하트 모양 쿠키
  - heart-shaped cookie
### Notes
- Related: 형태 (form), 크기 (size), 색깔 (color)
?end

## 형태 #card #form #shape #type #structure #computer
?begin
### 뜻
- form
- shape
- structure
- type
- format (e.g. of data/files)
### 예
- 형태가 다양해요
  - There are various forms/shapes
- 어떤 형태인가요?
  - What form is it?
- 물은 고체, 액체, 기체의 세 가지 형태로 존재해요
  - Water exists in three forms: solid, liquid, and gas
- 데이터의 형태를 바꾸다
  - change the format of data
- 다양한 형태로 나타나다
  - appear in various forms
- 파일 형태로 저장하다
  - save in file format
### Notes
- More formal than 모양 (the two are close in meaning)
- Related: 모양 (shape), 구조 (structure), 형식 (format)
?end

## 감촉 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- texture
- feel
- tactile sensation
### 예
- 감촉이 부드러워요
  - The texture is soft
- 천의 감촉이 좋다
  - The fabric has a nice feel
### Notes
- Close in meaning to 촉감
- Related: 촉감 (texture), 부드럽다 (soft), 거칠다 (rough)
?end

## 촉감 #card #touch #texture
?begin
### 뜻
- texture
- feel
- touch sensation
- touch
### 예
- 촉감이 좋아요
  - The texture/feel is nice
- 목재의 촉감
  - The feel of wood
- 촉감이 부드러워요
  - The texture is soft
- 좋은 촉감
  - Good touch
### Notes
- Close in meaning to 감촉
- Related: 감촉 (texture), 만지다 (touch), 부드럽다 (soft)
?end

## 재질 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- material (of an object)
- texture/composition of material
### 예
- 이건 무슨 재질이에요?
  - What material is this?
- 가죽 재질
  - leather material
### Notes
- More commonly used for materials of objects than 재료
- Related: 재료 (material/ingredient), 소재 (material), 성질 (property)
?end

## 네모나다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be square-shaped
- to be rectangular
- to be boxy
### 예
- 상자가 네모나요
  - The box is square/rectangular
- 이 책상은 네모난 모양이에요
  - This desk has a square/rectangular shape
### Notes
- 네모 = square/rectangle shape
- Related: 동그랗다 (round), 삼각형 (triangle), 모양 (shape)
?end
