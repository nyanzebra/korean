## 모양 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- shape
- form
- appearance

### 예
- 모양이 이뻐요
  - The shape is pretty
- 그게 무슨 모양이에요?
  - What shape is that?
- 모양 = 형태 (same meaning)
  - shape/form

### Notes
- Related: 형태 (form), 크기 (size), 색깔 (color)

?end

## 형태 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- form
- shape
- structure

### 예
- 형태가 다양해요
  - There are various forms/shapes
- 어떤 형태인가요?
  - What form/shape is it?
- 모양 = 형태 (same meaning)
  - shape/form

### Notes
- More formal than 모양
- Related: 모양 (shape), 구조 (structure), 형식 (format)

?end

## 재료 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- material
- ingredient
- raw material

### 예
- 이 옷의 재료가 뭐예요?
  - What's the material of this clothing?
- 재료 = 재질 (similar meaning for objects)
  - material
- 좋은 재료로 만들었어요
  - It's made with good materials

### Notes
- Used for: ingredients (cooking), materials (objects/clothing)
- Related: 재질 (material), 소재 (material), 성분 (component)

?end

## 네모나다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be square-shaped
- to be rectangular
- to be boxy

### 예
- 네모나다 = 네모난 편이다
  - to be square/rectangular-shaped
- 상자가 네모나요
  - The box is square/rectangular
- 이 책상은 네모난 모양이에요
  - This desk has a square/rectangular shape

### Notes
- 네모 = square shape, 나다 = appear/be
- Related: 동그란 (round), 삼각형 (triangle), 모양 (shape)

?end
