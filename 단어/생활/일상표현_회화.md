# 일상 표현 및 생활 용어 (Conversational Korean)

Collected from conversational learning materials.

## 식품 및 냉장고

### 유통기한
- **유통** - circulation of products
- **기한** - deadline, expiration
- **살짝** - slightly, a little bit
- **유통기한 살짝 지났어** - The expiration date has slightly passed

## 생활용품

### 환풍기 vs 선풍기
- **환풍기** - air ventilation fan (AC/exhaust fan)
- **선풍기** - regular fan (portable/table fan)
- **Example**: 선풍기 바람 이쪽으로 갈 수 있어? - Can we direct the fan wind this way?

### 꼼꼼히
- Definition: meticulously, carefully
- **오타 있는지 꼼꼼히 확인해** - Check carefully for typos

## 일상 행동 표현

### 얼버무리다
- Definition: slur, speak ambiguously, mumble
- **말 얼버무리지 마** - Don't speak ambiguously, speak clearly

### 까먹다
- Definition: forget
- **이따 하려고 하면 까먹어** - If you plan to do it later, you might forget

### 튀어나오다
- Definition: pop out, burst out
- **나도 모르게 웃음이 튀어나왔어** - I burst out laughing without realizing

### 둘러보다
- Definition: look around
- **둘러 본고 올게요** - I'll come and look around

### 말이 되다
- Definition: makes sense
- **이게 말이 된다고 생각해** - Do you think this makes sense?

## 추상 개념

### 기반 / 근본 / 기본
- **기반** - foundation, basis
- **근본** - root, original
- **기본** - basic, fundamental

### 자동
- Definition: automatic
