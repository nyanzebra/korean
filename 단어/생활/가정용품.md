## 담 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- low wall (often encircling a yard)
- fence-like wall around a house
### 예
- 집 둘레에 담을 쌓았어요
  - We built a wall around the house
- 고양이가 담 위를 걸어가요
  - The cat is walking along the top of the wall
### Notes
- Homonym: 담 also means "phlegm" (痰) or "courage/guts" (膽, as in 담력) — context decides
?end
