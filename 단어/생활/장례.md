## 장례 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- funeral
### 예
- 할아버지의 장례를 치렀어요
  - We held my grandfather's funeral
- 장례에 참석하려고 고향에 내려갔어요
  - I went down to my hometown to attend the funeral
?end

## 장례식을 하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to hold a funeral

### 예
- 다음 주에 할머니 장례식을 해요
  - We're holding my grandmother's funeral next week
- 장례식을 어디서 하기로 했어요?
  - Where did you decide to hold the funeral?

### Notes
- Related: 장례 (funeral), 장례식장 (funeral home)

?end

## 화장하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to cremate

### 예
- 할아버지를 화장하기로 했어요
  - We decided to cremate my grandfather
- 화장한 후에 유골을 납골당에 모셨어요
  - After the cremation, we placed the ashes in a columbarium

### Notes
- Homonym warning: 화장하다 also means "to put on makeup" (see 단어/몸/화장.md) — context determines the meaning
- Related: 화장시키다 (to have someone cremated), 유골 (ashes/remains)

?end

## 화장시키다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to have someone cremated
- to cause someone to be cremated

### 예
- 유언에 따라 아버지를 화장시켰어요
  - We had my father cremated according to his will

### Notes
- Causative form of 화장하다 (to cremate)

?end

?begin
### 뜻
- to embalm

### 예

## 애도하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to mourn
- to condole

### 예
- 그의 죽음을 애도했어요
  - We mourned his death
- 많은 사람들이 희생자들을 애도했어요
  - Many people mourned the victims

### Notes
- Related: 슬퍼하다 (to grieve/be sad), 조문하다 (to pay condolences)

?end

## 슬퍼할 시간이 필요하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to need time to grieve

### 예
- 가족을 잃고 슬퍼할 시간이 필요해요
  - After losing a family member, I need time to grieve

### Notes
- Related: 애도하다 (to mourn), 슬퍼하다 (to grieve)

?end
