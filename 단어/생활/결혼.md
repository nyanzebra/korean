# 결혼 (Marriage & Wedding)

This file contains vocabulary related to marriage, wedding ceremonies, and related concepts.

## 결혼 #card #relationship #marriage
?begin
### 뜻
- marriage
- wedding

### 예
- 결혼했어요
  - I got married
- 결혼식에 갔어요
  - I went to a wedding
- 친구가 다음 달에 결혼해요
  - My friend is getting married next month

### Notes
- 결혼식: wedding ceremony
?end

## 결혼하다 #card #relationship #marry
?begin
### 뜻
- to marry
- to get married

### 예
- 작년에 결혼했어요
  - I got married last year
- 결혼할 거예요
  - I will get married
- 내년에 결혼할 거예요
  - I'm going to get married next year
?end

## 장가 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- marriage (for men)
### 예
- 남자는 장가를 가다
  - a man gets married
- 우리 형은 작년에 장가를 갔어요
  - My older brother got married last year
### Notes
- 장가(를) 가다 / 장가들다 = (a man) to get married
- Female counterpart: 시집 (시집을 가다)
?end

## 시집 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- marriage (for women)
- one's husband's family/home (시집 = 시댁)
### 예
- 여자는 시집을 가다
  - a woman gets married
- 언니는 스물여덟 살에 시집을 갔어요
  - My older sister got married at twenty-eight
### Notes
- 시집(을) 가다 = (a woman) to get married
- Male counterpart: 장가 (장가를 가다)
- Homonym: 시집 (詩集) also means a poetry collection
?end

## 이혼 #card #relationship #divorce
?begin
### 뜻
- divorce
### 예
- 이혼했어요
  - I got divorced
- 이혼할 거예요
  - I will get divorced
- 부모님이 이혼하셨어요
  - My parents got divorced
- 이혼 후에 혼자 살고 있어요
  - I have been living alone since the divorce
### Notes
- 이혼하다: to get divorced
?end

## 신랑 #card #person #groom #sogang #sogang-4A

?begin
### 뜻
- bride
### 예

  - beautiful bride
- 신부가 들어왔어요

## 결혼 증명서 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- marriage certificate
- certificate of marriage

### 예
- 결혼 증명서를 내야 해요
  - I need to submit a marriage certificate
- 결혼 증명서를 받았어요
  - I received a marriage certificate

### Notes
- Related: 출생 증명서 (birth certificate), 증명 (proof), 행정 (administration)
?end
