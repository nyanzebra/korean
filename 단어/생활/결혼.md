# 결혼 (Marriage & Wedding)

This file contains vocabulary related to marriage, wedding ceremonies, and related concepts.

## 결혼 #card #relationship #marriage
?begin
### 뜻
- marriage
### 예
- 결혼했어요	I got married
- 결혼식에 갔어요	I went to a wedding
?end

?begin

### 예

- 결혼할 거예요	I will get married
?end
