
## 천천히 #card #adverb #slowly
?begin
### 뜻
- slowly
- at a slow pace
- gently
- leisurely

### 예
- 천천히 해도 되니까 서두르지 마
  - It's okay to go slowly, so don't hurry
- 천천히 달리고 있어
  - I'm running slowly
- 천천히 말씀해 주세요
  - Please speak slowly
- 천천히 가요
  - I go slowly
- 천천히 말하세요
  - Speak slowly

### Notes
- Related: 느리게 (slowly), 서서히 (gradually), 침착하게 (calmly)

?end

## 잠시 #card #time #moment
?begin
### 뜻
- for a moment
- for a while
- temporarily
- briefly

### 예
- 잠시 움직이지 말고 가만히 있어
  - Don't move, stay still for a moment
- 잠시만요
  - Just a moment please
- 잠시 기다려 주세요
  - Please wait a moment
- 잠시만 기다리세요
  - Wait a moment please
- 잠시 후
  - In a moment

### Notes
- Related: 순간 (instant), 한참 (quite a while), 아주 잠깐 (just a second)

?end

## 가만히 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- still
- motionless
- quietly
- calmly

### 예
- 잠시 움직이지 말고 가만히 있어
  - Stay still for a moment, don't move
- 가만히 앉아서 관찰하고 있었어
  - I was sitting quietly observing
- 가만히 있어 봐, 생각났어
  - Hold on, just a second - I remembered

### Notes
- Related: 조용히 (quietly), 가만 (just as it is), 움직이지 않다 (don't move)

?end

## 언젠가 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- sometime
- someday
- at some point
- one day

### 예
- 언젠가 내가 말했지?
  - Didn't I tell you sometime?
- 언젠가는 해결 될 거야
  - It will be resolved someday
- 언젠가 이긴 적 있어
  - I won once at some point
- 우리 언젠가 만난 적 있어?
  - Have we met before at some point?

### Notes
- Related: 언젠간 (someday), 나중 (later), 그날 (that day)

?end
