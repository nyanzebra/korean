## 의논하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to discuss
- to consult
- to confer
- to talk (something over)
### 예
- 급한 마당에 의논할 여유가 없었어
  - In such an urgent situation, I didn't have time to discuss
- 부모님과 진로를 의논했어요
  - I discussed my career path with my parents
- 측근과 의논하다
	- consult one's aides.
- 변호사와 의논하다
	- consult a lawyer.
### Notes
- Related: 상담하다 (consult), 논의하다 (discuss), 협의하다 (confer)
- More like "discuss with" someone
<!--SR:!2026-12-22,138,249-->
?end
