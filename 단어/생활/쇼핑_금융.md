## 챙기다 #card #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- take
- take care of
- bring
- pack
- pocket (money)
### 예
- 자신의 고등학교 학위증을 챙겼다고요. 나중에 그는 이유를 말했습니다.
	- He took his high school diploma, and later he told me why.
- 엄마는 먹을 것과 잠, 관심에 대한 우리의 필요를 챙겨 주셨다.
	- She took care of our needs for food, sleep and attention.
- 내 가방을 챙겨올게
	- Let me just go get my bag.
- 이 과정에서 법원과 전관예우로 연결된 로펌과 변호사들이 사건을 맡아 막대한 수임료를 챙겼다.
	- In the process, law firms and lawyers connected to the court through preferential treatment for former officials took on cases and pocketed huge fees.
- 걸칠 거리라도 좀 챙겨올 걸 그랬네.
	- I should have brought something to throw on.
- 제가 아빠 물건들을 챙길게요
	- I'll pack up your stuff.
### Notes
- differs from 가지다 in that this has more emphasis on bring/taking something well/securely
### Tags
#shopping #packing #bringing #taking-care #organizing #preparation
?end
