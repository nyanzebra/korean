## 따로따로 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- separately
- individually
### 예
- 계산은 따로따로 해 주세요
  - Please make the bills separately
- 우리는 같은 모임에 갔지만 따로따로 도착했어요

## (을/를) 대하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to treat (a person)
- to deal with (a person)
- to face (someone/something)
### 예
- 그는 손님을 늘 친절하게 대해요
  - He always treats customers kindly
- 부모님을 공손하게 대해야 해요
  - You should treat your parents respectfully

## (을/를) 취급하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to treat (as in "to handle / to regard as")
- to handle (things)
### 예
- 이 물건은 깨지기 쉬우니까 조심해서 취급해 주세요
  - This item is fragile, so please handle it with care
- 그는 저를 어린아이처럼 취급해요
  - He treats me like a child
### Notes
- Used mainly for things (goods, materials) or for regarding someone/something "as" something; for people's behavior toward others, see (을/를) 대하다
- Related: 다루다 (to handle, deal with)
?end

## 부하 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- subordinate (部下)
- follower (部下)
- load, burden (負荷)
### 예
- 그는 부하가 자신의 비밀을 알고 있어서 부하에게 약점을 잡혀 있다
  - He is in the power of his subordinate, who knows his secret
- 기계에 너무 큰 부하가 걸려서 멈췄어요
  - The machine stopped because too great a load was placed on it
### Notes
- Two different hanja words with the same sound: 部下 (subordinate) and 負荷 (load)
- Related: 상사 (superior)
?end
