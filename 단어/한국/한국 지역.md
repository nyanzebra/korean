## 설악산 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- Seoraksan (Mt. Seorak, a famous national park in Gangwon-do)
### 예
- 가을에 설악산에 단풍을 보러 갔어요.
  - In autumn I went to Seoraksan to see the autumn leaves.
- 설악산에는 십이선녀탕 계곡이 있어요.
  - Seoraksan has the Twelve Fairy Baths valley (십이선녀탕).
### Notes
- 십이선녀탕 = a scenic valley with waterfalls (폭포) and pools; 복숭아탕 is one of its named pools
- Mountain names end in 산: 한라산, 북한산, 지리산
- Related: 단풍을 구경하다 (see [[식물과 과일]])
?end
