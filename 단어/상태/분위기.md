## 선선하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- pleasantly cool
- refreshing (of air or breeze)
- frank, easygoing (of manner)
### 예
- 가을이 되니 아침저녁으로 바람이 선선하네요
  - Now that it is autumn, the breeze is cool in the morning and evening
- 그는 내 부탁을 선선하게 들어주었다
  - He readily granted my request
### Notes
- Related: 시원하다 (be cool/refreshing), 쌀쌀하다 (be chilly)
### Tags
#cool #refreshing #weather #temperature #frank #manner

?end
