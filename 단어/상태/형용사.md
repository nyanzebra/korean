## 트이다 / 탁 트이다 #card #adjective #open #wide #sogang #sogang-4A
?begin
### 뜻
- to open up; to be cleared
- to be wide open (탁 트이다)
- to be unobstructed
- to have a clear view
### 예
- 휴가 기간이 지나서 가니까 복잡하지 않아서 좋더라고요. 탁 트인 고속도로를 달리는 것도 좋았고요
  - It was good because it wasn't crowded since the vacation period was over. It was also nice to drive on the wide open highway
- 탁 트인 전망이 좋아요
  - The unobstructed view is nice
- 전망이 트이다
  - the view opens up
- 시야가 트이는 곳
  - a place with an open view
- 숨통이 트이다
  - to finally be able to breathe freely (to get some relief)
### Notes
- 탁 (adverb) emphasizes openness: 탁 트이다 = wide open, with nothing in the way
- Related: 시원하다 (be refreshing), 막히다 (be blocked, opposite)
?end

## 살짝 #card #adverb
?begin
### 뜻
- slightly
- a little
- lightly
- secretly, on the quiet
### 예
- 꽃잎 끝 부분이 살짝 벌어졌어
  - The tip of the flower petal has slightly opened
- 살짝 웃다
  - to smile slightly
- 살짝 건드리다
  - to touch lightly
- 그 사람 몰래 살짝 알려 줬어요
  - I quietly let him know without the others noticing
### Notes
- 살짝 = lightly / slightly / on the sly; 살살 = gently, softly (repeated light movement): 살살 만지세요 (Touch it gently)
- Related: 조금 (a little), 살며시 (softly, quietly), 몰래 (secretly)
?end
