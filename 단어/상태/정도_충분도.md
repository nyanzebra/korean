## 충분해 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- enough
- sufficient
- that's enough (informal)

### 예
- 그만큼이면 충분해
  - That much is enough
- 돈이 충분해요
  - I have enough money
- 충분해, 더는 안 돼
  - That's enough, no more

### Notes
- Related: 충분하다 (be enough), 넉넉하다 (plenty), 부족하다 (insufficient)

?end

## 충분하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be enough
- to be sufficient
- to be adequate

### 예
- 그만큼이면 충분하다
  - That much is enough
- 시간이 충분해요
  - There's enough time
- 충분한 준비
  - sufficient preparation

### Notes
- Related: 충분함 (sufficiency), 넉넉하다 (be plenty), 모자라다 (be insufficient)

?end

## 여유 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- spare time/space
- room
- afford
- margin

### 예
- 급한 마당에 의논할 여유가 없었어
  - In such an urgent situation, I didn't have time to discuss
- 시간 여유가 있어
  - I have spare time
- 여유를 가지다
  - to have some room/margin
- 금전 여유
  - financial margin

### Notes
- Related: 시간 (time), 여유로운 (relaxed), 넉넉하다 (be plenty)

?end
