## 옳다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be right
- to be correct
- to be proper
- to be fair

### 예
- 그의 말이 옳아요
  - What he said is right
- 그게 옳은 방법이에요
  - That's the correct method
- 옳은 판단
  - correct judgment
- 옳고 그름
  - right and wrong

### Notes
- Related: 맞다 (be correct), 올바르다 (be proper), 정당하다 (be just)

?end
