## 불운 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- misfortune
- bad luck
- unlucky/unfortunate (situation)
### 예
- 불운을 겪다
  - To encounter misfortune
- 불운하게도
  - Unfortunately
### Notes
- Source sentence: "힘으로 유지되어야 할 필요가 있는 것은 무엇이나 불운이다" - Anything that needs to be maintained by force is unfortunate.
- Related: 유감스럽다 (be regrettable), 운 (luck)
?end

## 새우등 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- shrimp back
- hunched back
### 예
- 새우등처럼 굽어 있다
  - to be bent like a shrimp's back
- 오래 앉아 있었더니 새우등이 됐어요
  - After sitting for so long, my back became hunched
### Notes
- From the proverb "고래 싸움에 새우 등 터진다" (when whales fight, the shrimp's back breaks) - the weak suffer from the quarrels of the strong; see 표현_관용구/속담.md
- Related: 터지다 (to burst), 방관자 (bystander)
?end
