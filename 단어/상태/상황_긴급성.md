## 급하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be urgent
- to be pressing
- to be hurried

### 예
- 급한 마당에 의논할 여유가 없었어
  - In such an urgent situation, I didn't have time to discuss
- 급한 일이 있어요
  - I have urgent business
- 너무 급해하지 마
  - Don't be in such a hurry
- 급한 경우
  - in case of emergency

### Notes
- Related: 긴급 (emergency), 서두르다 (hurry), 위급 (critical)

?end
