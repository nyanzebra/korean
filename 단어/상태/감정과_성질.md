## 변덕스럽다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be fickle
- to be changeable
- to be capricious

### 예
- 기분이 변덕스럽다
  - to have a fickle/changeable mood
- 성격이 변덕스럽다
  - to have a capricious personality
- 날씨가 변덕스럽다
  - the weather is unpredictable/changeable
- 기쁘다가 슬프다가 기분이 변덕스러웠어요
  - My mood changed fickly between happy and sad

### Notes
- Related: 기분파 (moody person), 일관성 (consistency)

?end
