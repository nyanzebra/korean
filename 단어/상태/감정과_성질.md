## 변덕스럽다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be fickle
- to be changeable
- to be capricious

### 예
- 기분이 변덕스럽다
  - to have a fickle/changeable mood
- 성격이 변덕스럽다
  - to have a capricious personality
- 날씨가 변덕스럽다
  - the weather is unpredictable/changeable
- 기쁘다가 슬프다가 기분이 변덕스러웠어요
  - My mood changed fickly between happy and sad

### Notes
- Related: 기분파 (moody person), 일관성 (consistency)

?end

## 당황하다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be embarrassed
- to be startled
- to be bewildered
- to be taken aback

### 예
- 당황했어요
  - I was embarrassed/startled
- 그 소식에 당황했어요
  - I was taken aback by that news
- 황당하다 (colloquial variation)
  - to be absurd/ridiculous

### Notes
- Synonyms: 황당하다 (more casual), 놀라다 (be surprised)

?end

## 헷갈리다 #card
?begin
### 뜻

- to be bewildered

### 예
- 헷갈렸어요
  - I was confused/mixed up
- 비슷한 단어들이 헷갈려요

### Notes

?end

## 어이가 없다 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be dumbfounded
- to be taken aback
- to find it ridiculous

### 예
- 어이가 없었어요
  - I was dumbfounded/taken aback
- 그 말을 듣고 어이가 없었어요
  - I was taken aback when I heard that

### Notes
- Related: 황당하다 (be absurd), 놀라다 (be surprised), 당황하다 (be startled)
