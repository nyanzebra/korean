## 유죄 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- guilty
- guilty verdict
- conviction

### 예
- 유죄 판결
  - guilty verdict
- 그를 유죄로 판단했어
  - He was judged guilty
- 유죄인지 무죄인지 판결 중이야
  - We're deciding if (he's) guilty or not guilty

### Notes
- Related: 죄 (crime), 무죄 (innocent), 판결 (judgment)

?end

## 무죄 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- innocent
- not guilty
- acquittal

### 예
- 무죄 판결
  - acquittal/not guilty verdict
- 그는 무죄예요
  - He is innocent
- 무죄로 풀려났어요
  - He was released with acquittal

### Notes
- Related: 죄 (crime), 유죄 (guilty), 판결 (judgment)

?end
