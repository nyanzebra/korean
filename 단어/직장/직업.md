## 점원 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- shop assistant
- store clerk
- sales person

### 예
- 점원이 도와줬어요
  - The shop assistant helped me
- 점원 = 직원 (related but 직원 is more general)
  - shop assistant / staff member

### Notes
- Related: 직원 (employee/staff), 판매원 (sales person), 캐셔 (cashier)

?end

## 우체부 #card
?begin
### 뜻
- postal worker
- mail carrier
- postman

### 예
- 우체부가 편지를 배달했어요
  - The mail carrier delivered the letter
- 우체부 = 우편물 배달하는 사람 = 우체국 직원
  - postal worker / mail carrier / post office employee

### Notes
- Related: 우편 (mail), 우체국 (post office), 배달 (delivery)

?end
