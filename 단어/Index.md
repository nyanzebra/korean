## 🎯 **Usage Recommendations**

### For Beginners:
1. Start with [[단词/表現_慣用句/기본_인사말|기본 인사말]] (Basic greetings)
2. Move to [[單詞/表現_慣用句/일상표현|일상 표현]] (Daily expressions)
3. Learn [[단词/활동]] and [[단词/食事]] (Activities and food)

### For Intermediate Learners:
1. Review [[単語/表現_慣用句|表現_慣用句]] (Expressions and idioms)
2. Study [[単語/形容詞|形容詞]] and [[单词/動詞|動詞]] (Adjectives and verbs)
3. Explore [[単語/推象的な物]] categories relevant to your interests

### For Advanced Learners:
1. Deep dive into [[単語/推象的な物]] (Abstract concepts)
2. Study [[単語/文法|文法]] in the grammar folder (if studying grammar)
3. Review [[単語/表現_慣用句/속담|속담]] and [[単語/表現_慣用句/格言_名言|格言 & 名言]] (Traditional sayings)

### By Study Goal:
- **Conversational:** Focus on 표현_관용구, 활동, 생활
- **Writing:** Study 동사, 형용사, 추상적인 것
- **Listening/Listening Comprehension:** Review 表現_慣用句 and natural speech patterns
- **TOPIK Exam:** Systematically cover all semantic categories

## 📝 **File Organization Strategy**

### Semantic Categories
Each folder represents a meaningful concept area rather than part of speech:
- **세상** (The world) - Nature and global topics
- **생활** (Daily life) - Practical everyday vocabulary
- **추상적인 것** (Abstract) - Ideas, emotions, concepts

### Flashcard Format
All files use Obsidian-compatible flashcard format with:
- `#card` markers for spaced repetition
- Multiple example sentences
- English translations
- Related terms and comparisons

### Cross-References
- Files link to related semantic categories
- Grammar structures marked with `~`
- Formal/casual distinctions noted where relevant
