# 불운과 고충 (Misfortune & Hardship)

Idioms about hardship, anxiety, balance and the ups and downs of fortune.

## 고진감래 (苦盡甘來) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- sweet after bitter (pleasure follows pain; no pain, no gain)
- 괴로움이 다하면 즐거움이 찾아옴

### 예
- 고진감래라더니, 몇 년 고생한 끝에 드디어 취직했어요
  - As they say, sweet comes after bitter, and after years of struggle I finally got a job
- 지금은 힘들어도 고진감래니까 조금만 참아요
  - It's hard now, but good things come after hardship, so hang in there a little longer

### Notes
- Related proverb: 고생 끝에 낙이 온다
?end

## 과유불급 (過猶不及) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- too much is as bad as too little
- 정도를 지나치면 미치지 못한 것과 같음

### 예
- 운동도 과유불급이니까 무리하지 마세요
  - Even exercise shouldn't be overdone, so don't push yourself
- 약도 과유불급이에요, 너무 많이 먹으면 오히려 해로워요
  - Even medicine follows the rule of moderation; too much is harmful instead

### Notes
- From the Analects of Confucius (論語)

## 새옹지마 (塞翁之馬) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- "a blessing in disguise"; fortune and misfortune are unpredictable and intertwined
- what seems like bad luck may turn out well, and vice versa

### 예
- 인생은 새옹지마예요
  - Life is like saeongjima (you never know if something is a blessing or a curse)
- 그 일이 새옹지마가 될 줄 누가 알았겠어요
  - Who would have known that would turn out to be a blessing in disguise

### Notes
- 새옹 (塞翁): an old man at the frontier; 지 (之): possessive particle; 마 (馬): horse
- From a story in which an old man's horse ran away (seemingly bad), then returned with another horse (seemingly good), and so on, illustrating that fortune and misfortune are hard to judge in the moment
?end

## 노심초사 (勞心焦思) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to be deeply anxious and worried (great anxiety, taking great pains)
- 애를 쓰며 속을 태움

### 예
- 아들이 돌아오지 않아서 부모님이 노심초사하고 계세요
  - My parents are deeply worried because their son hasn't come back
- 시험 결과를 기다리며 노심초사했어요
  - I was on tenterhooks waiting for the exam results

### Notes
- Used as a verb: 노심초사하다
