# 성공과 성취 (Success & Achievement)

Idioms about success, good fortune and honor.

## 금의환향 (錦衣還鄕) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to return home in glory (to make an honorable return, to return with success)
- 출세하여 고향에 돌아옴

### 예
- 그는 올림픽에서 금메달을 따고 금의환향했어요
  - He won a gold medal at the Olympics and returned home in glory
- 성공해서 금의환향하겠다는 다짐으로 고향을 떠났어요
  - He left his hometown, determined to return in triumph

### Notes
- Spelling: 금의환향 (not 금의환양); 환향 (還鄕) = to return to one's hometown
- Literally: returning home wearing embroidered silk clothes

## 금상첨화 (錦上添花) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- adding flowers to embroidery (the icing on the cake)
- 좋은 일이 겹침

### 예
- 월급도 많고 집에서도 가까우니 금상첨화예요
  - The salary is high and it's close to home, which is the icing on the cake
- 맛있는 음식에 멋진 경치까지, 그야말로 금상첨화네요
  - Delicious food and a wonderful view too, that's really the icing on the cake

### Notes
- Opposite: 설상가상 (雪上加霜): misfortune upon misfortune
