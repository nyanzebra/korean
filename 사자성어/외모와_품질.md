# 외모와 품질 (Appearance & Quality)

Idioms about beauty and things that are precious.

## 경국지색 (傾國之色) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- a beauty so great she could topple a country (a Helen of Troy)
- 나라의 운명을 위태롭게 할 만한 절세의 미인

### 예
- 그 배우는 경국지색이라고 불릴 만큼 아름다워요
  - That actress is so beautiful she's called a beauty who could topple a nation
- 옛이야기에는 경국지색 때문에 나라가 망했다는 내용이 많아요
  - Old stories often say a kingdom fell because of a ravishing beauty

?end

## 금지옥엽 (金枝玉葉) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- "golden branch, jade leaf" (a precious child, the apple of one's eye)
- 자손을 소중히 여겨 일컫는 말

### 예
- 그에게 딸은 금지옥엽이에요
  - To him, his daughter is the apple of his eye
- 금지옥엽처럼 키운 아들이 드디어 졸업했어요
  - The son they raised like a precious jewel has finally graduated
?end
