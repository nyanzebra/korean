## 📖 Idioms by Semantic Category

### 🏃 **[[ㄱ|행동과 성격]] (Behavior & Character)**
Idioms expressing behavior, character traits, habits, and personal qualities.

**Key Concepts:**
- Personal habits and behavioral patterns
- Character development and personality
- Deliberate actions and intentions
- How people conduct themselves

Examples include: 작심삼일 (three-day resolution), 근묵자흑 (ink rubs off black), and similar expressions about character and behavior.

---

### 👥 **[[ㄴ|인간관계]] (Human Relationships)**
Idioms expressing relationships, friendships, loyalty, and social bonds.

**Key Concepts:**
- Friendships and bonds between people
- Trust and loyalty in relationships
- Social connections and obligations
- How people relate to each other

Examples include: 관포지교 (pipe and knife friendship), 수어지교 (glove and fish friendship), and similar expressions about relationships.

## 📅 Organization Notes

**Philosophy:** 
Following the repository's semantic organization principle (see [[../AGENT.md|AGENT.md]]), idioms are grouped by **meaning and context** rather than alphabetical order. This allows:

✅ Learning idioms in thematic clusters  
✅ Understanding related concepts together  
✅ Discovering similar expressions naturally  
✅ Connecting cultural and linguistic contexts  

**Future Expansion:**
As the collection grows, consider organizing further by these semantic categories:
- **Success & Achievement** (금의환양, 금상첨화)
- **Misfortune & Hardship** (고진감래, 과유불급)
- **Appearance & Quality** (경국지색, 금지옥엽)
- **Education & Learning** (권학문
- **Wisdom & Foolishness** (대기만성, 대동소이)
