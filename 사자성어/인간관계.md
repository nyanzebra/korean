# 인간관계 (Human Relationships)

Idioms about friendship, trust and social bonds.

## 관포지교 (管鮑之交) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- an extremely close, lifelong friendship (a David and Jonathan friendship)
- 우정이 깊은 사귐

### 예
- 우리 두 사람은 관포지교처럼 서로를 믿는 사이예요
  - The two of us trust each other like true lifelong friends
- 그들은 어릴 때부터 관포지교를 나눈 친구예요
  - They have been the closest of friends since childhood

### Notes
- From the friendship of Guan Zhong (管仲) and Bao Shuya (鮑叔牙) in ancient China
- Synonyms: 지란지교 (芝蘭之交), 수어지교 (水魚之交: friendship like water and fish)
?end
