# 행동과 성격 (Behavior & Character)

Idioms about habits, character traits and the way people behave.

## 작심삼일 (作心三日) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- a resolution that lasts only three days
- giving up soon after making up one's mind

### 예
- 매년 새해에 세운 계획이 작심삼일로 끝나요
  - My New Year's resolutions end after three days every year
- 헬스장을 끊었지만 작심삼일이 될까 봐 걱정이에요
  - I signed up for a gym, but I'm worried it will fizzle out after three days

### Notes
- 작심 (作心): to make up one's mind, to resolve; 삼일 (三日): three days
- Reflects the human tendency to lose motivation quickly

## 근묵자흑 (近墨者黑) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- He who touches pitch shall be defiled (one easily takes on the bad habits of bad company)
- 악한 사람을 가까이하면 그 버릇에 물들기 쉬움

### 예
- 근묵자흑이라고, 나쁜 친구들과 어울리다 보니 그도 변했어요
  - As they say, you are shaped by the company you keep, and he changed after hanging around bad friends
- 아이가 나쁜 습관에 물들지 않도록 근묵자흑을 조심해야 해요
  - We must be careful about bad influences so the child doesn't pick up bad habits

### Notes
- Literally: whoever is near ink turns black
- Related: 근주자적 (近朱者赤): whoever is near vermilion turns red (good company has a good influence)

## 내유외강 (內柔外剛) / 외강내유 (外剛內柔) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- soft on the inside, tough on the outside
- 속마음은 약한데도 겉으로는 강하게 나타남

### 예
- 그 사람은 겉으로는 무뚝뚝하지만 사실은 외강내유예요
  - He seems gruff on the outside, but he is actually soft on the inside
- 외강내유라서 화는 잘 내도 금방 후회해요
  - He is tough on the outside but soft on the inside, so he gets angry easily but regrets it quickly

### Notes
- Its opposite: 외유내강 (外柔內剛): gentle on the outside, strong on the inside

?end

## 감언이설 (甘言利說) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- sweet talk; honeyed words meant to deceive
- 남의 비위에 맞게 달콤한 말로 꾀는 말

### 예
- 감언이설에 속아서 투자했다가 돈을 잃었어요
  - I was taken in by smooth talk, invested, and lost money
- 그 사람의 감언이설에 넘어가지 마세요
  - Don't fall for his sweet talk

### Notes
- 감언 (甘言): sweet words; 이설 (利說): profitable-sounding talk
?end

## 침소봉대 (針小棒大) #card
?begin
### 뜻
- to make a pole out of a needle (to exaggerate, to make a mountain out of a molehill)

### 예
- 작은 일을 침소봉대해서 말하지 마세요
  - Don't exaggerate small matters
- 기자가 사건을 침소봉대해서 보도했어요
  - The reporter blew the incident out of proportion in his report

### Notes
- Used as a verb: 침소봉대하다
?end
