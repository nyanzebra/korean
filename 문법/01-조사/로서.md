## (으)로서 #card
?begin
### Usage
Establishes status, position, or authority ("as a...")

### Form
- Noun ending in vowel + 로서
- Noun ending in consonant + 으로서

### Restrictions
- Adverb marker
- Attaches to nouns only
- Can be replaced with simple (으)로 in casual speech

### Examples
- 학생으로서 = 학생으로
	- As a student...
- 자식으로서 할 일 = 자식으로 할 일
	- Something one does as a child
- 학자로서 = 학자로
	- As a scholar...
- 교사로서 책임을 다하겠습니다
	- As a teacher, I will fulfill my responsibilities

### Notes
?end
