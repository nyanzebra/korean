## 게 되다/됐어요 #card
?begin
### Usage
Expresses that a situation came about as a result of circumstances, not through the subject's own
direct will or decision — "it turned out that...", "ended up ...ing", "came to be...". Often used to
soften an announcement (e.g. of a job change, move, marriage) by framing it as something that
happened to the speaker rather than something they actively chose.
### Form
Verb stem + -게 되다 (no vowel harmony needed — 게 always attaches the same way)
가다 → 가게 되다, 살다 → 살게 되다, 만나다 → 만나게 되다
Past: -게 됐어요 (게 되다 + 았어요 → 되었어요 → 됐어요)
### Restrictions
- Mostly used with action verbs; less common with descriptive verbs/adjectives (compare with
  [[아 어 지다 (상태 변화)]] which is the equivalent pattern for adjectives — "become [adjective]").
- Implies the change was not entirely the subject's own doing/decision — for something you clearly
  chose or did on purpose, plain past tense or -기로 하다 ("decided to") is more natural.
### Examples
* 다음 달에 회사를 그만두게 됐어요.
	* It turned out that I'll be quitting my job next month. (softening an announcement)
* 한국에서 3년 동안 살게 됐어요.
	* I ended up living in Korea for 3 years.
* 우연히 옛날 친구를 만나게 됐어요.
	* I happened to run into an old friend.
* 어렸을 때는 매운 음식을 못 먹었는데 이제는 잘 먹게 됐어요.
	* When I was young I couldn't eat spicy food, but now I've come to be able to eat it well.
* 사정이 있어서 이사하게 됐어요.
	* Due to circumstances, I ended up moving.
### Notes
Compare with [[서..게 됐어요]] for a combined pattern, and [[아 어 지다 (상태 변화)]] for the
equivalent "become ___" pattern used with adjectives rather than action verbs.
?end
