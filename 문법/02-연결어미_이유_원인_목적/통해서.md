## 통해서 #card
?begin
### Usage
"Through / via / by means of NOUN"
1. Physical path: something passes through a place
2. Medium or channel: how information, goods, or contact travel
3. Experience or process: what you gain from going through it
4. Intermediary: a person or thing that connects you to something
### Form
N을/를 통해서 (short: 통해, formal: 통하여)
Modifier: N을/를 통한 + N (인터넷을 통한 주문 = ordering via the internet)
### Restrictions
* Attaches to NOUNS only. For a verb, nominalize first: V는 것을 통해서
* Not for reasons or causes. Use 때문에 or 아/어서 (✗ 사고를 통해서 늦었어요 → ✓ 사고 때문에 늦었어요)
* Not for tools, materials, direction, or status. Use (으)로 (see Notes)
### Examples
* 창문을 통해서 햇빛이 들어와요
  Sunlight comes in through the window.
* 인터넷을 통해서 주문했어요
  I ordered it through the internet.
* 친구를 통해서 그 회사를 알게 됐어요
  I found out about that company through a friend.
* 이번 여행을 통해서 많은 걸 알게 됐어요
  I learned a lot through this trip.
### Notes
**Core difference from (으)로**
* (으)로 = the thing you USE (tool, material, method, direction, status)
* 통해서 = the thing you PASS THROUGH (channel, route, intermediary, process)
* Quick test: if "using" fits, it's (으)로. If "through / via" fits, it's 통해서.

**Where they overlap (either works)**
* Physical paths: 창문으로 / 창문을 통해서 햇빛이 들어와요. (으)로 is more natural in speech.
* Media and channels: 인터넷으로 / 인터넷을 통해서 주문했어요. (으)로 = the means you used. 통해서 = the route it traveled, a bit more formal.

**Where only 통해서 works**
* Experience or process: 여행을 통해서 배웠어요 ✓ / 여행으로 배웠어요 ✗ (sounds like "by means of a trip" or "due to a trip")
* Intermediary (a go-between): 친구를 통해서 알게 됐어요 = through a friend, who connected me.
* Bridge language: 영어를 통해서 한국어를 배웠어요 = I learned Korean via English (English was the go-between).

**Where only (으)로 works**
* Tool: 칼로 잘랐어요 (✗ 칼을 통해서)
* Material: 나무로 만들었어요 (✗ 나무를 통해서)
* Direction: 서울로 가요 (✗ 서울을 통해서)
* Language of communication: 영어로 말해요 = I speak in English (✗ 영어를 통해서)

**Same noun, different meaning**
* 친구로 = as a friend (status): 친구로 지내요 = We get along as friends.
* 친구를 통해서 = via a friend (intermediary): 친구를 통해서 만났어요 = We met through a friend.
* 영어로 배웠어요 = I learned it in English (English was the language of instruction).
* 영어를 통해서 배웠어요 = I learned it via English (English was a stepping stone).

**Register**
통해서 feels more written and formal. In speech, (으)로 replaces it for paths and media, but NOT for experiences and intermediaries.
