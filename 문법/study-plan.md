# Korean Grammar Study Plan

A suggested path through this vault, loosely following TOPIK I → II → intermediate/advanced
progression. Check off items as you cover them. Items marked **(stub)** live in
`00-기초 필수 문법 (TODO stubs)/` and need content filled in — treat them as a "add detail when you
get here" reminder rather than a finished card.
