## 게 하다 (make / let) #card
?begin
### Usage
Indirect causative: "to make someone do ~" or "to let someone do ~". The same form covers both meanings; context decides which one is meant. The subject orders/allows/causes someone else to do the action (rather than physically doing it to them — compare the causative verbs in [[causative]]).
- **V-게 하다** — "make" or "let"
- **V-게 해 주다** — "let (me/someone) do ~" as a favour (permission)
- **V-게 만들다** — only "make" (cause, force; uses 만들다 "to make")
### Form
Verb stem + **게** + conjugated 하다 / 해 주다 / 만들다.
| Pattern | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| V-게 하다 | make or let (from context) |
| V-게 해 주다 | let / allow as a favour (해 주다 = "do as a favour"; GO! Billy Korean Ep. 36 "I'll do it for you") |
| V-게 만들다 | make only |
| N-을/를 시키다 | make someone do N (시키다 replaces 하다) |
### Restrictions
- Use with action verbs (the lesson stresses that these are action verbs, not adjectives/descriptive verbs); 화나다/싸우다 style verbs work.
- 게 하다 itself can mean "make" or "let"; if you need to be unambiguous about "make" (force/cause), use 게 만들다.
- 시키다 can only replace the verb 하다 (e.g. 일을 시키다, 청소를 시키다). For all other verbs use -게 하다 / -게 만들다.
- "피자(를) 시키다" is a separate meaning of 시키다: "to order a pizza".
- 좀 is an abbreviation of 조금 ("a little"); it softens requests (저 좀 자게 해 주세요).
### Examples
#### V-게 하다
* 강아지가 초콜릿을 먹게 하지 마.
	* Don't let the puppy eat chocolate. (could also be "make", but "let" is more sensible here)
* 내가 또 말하게 하지 마.
	* Don't make me say it again.
#### V-게 해 주다
* 저 좀 자게 해 주세요.
	* Please let me sleep.
* 티비를 볼 수 있게 해 주세요.
	* Literal: Please let me be able to watch TV. / Meaning: Please let me watch TV.
#### V-게 만들다
* 철수를 화나게 만들었어.
	* I made Cheolsoo get angry. (화나다 = to get angry)
* 친구들이 싸우게 만들었어.
	* I made my friends fight. (싸우다 = to fight)
#### 시키다 (advanced)
* 일을 시키다
	* to make someone work
* 청소를 시키다
	* to make someone do cleaning
* 피자(를) 시키다
	* to order a pizza
### Notes
- Source: GO! Billy Korean, "Learn Korean Ep. 45: Make & Let".
- Direct vs indirect causation (see [[causative]]): 엄마가 아기에게 밥을 먹여요 (먹이다 — mum feeds the baby by hand) vs 엄마가 아이에게 밥을 먹게 했어요 (게 하다 — mum told the child to eat by themselves).
- Related: [[passive]] (the other voice-changing pattern), [[어 주다]] (doing something as a favour, used in 게 해 주다).
