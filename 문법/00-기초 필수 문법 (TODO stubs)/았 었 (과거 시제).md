## 았/었 (past tense) #card
?begin
### Usage
The basic past tense marker, attached directly to the verb/adjective stem before the sentence-final
ending. Indicates that an action or state occurred/was true before the moment of speaking.
### Form
| Stem vowel | Marker |
| --- | --- |
| ㅏ, ㅗ | -았- |
| other vowels | -었- |
| 하다 | -했- (from 하 + 였 → 했) |

Combined with polite ending -어요: 갔어요, 먹었어요, 했어요.
Combined with formal ending -습니다: 갔습니다, 먹었습니다, 했습니다.
### Restrictions
- With 이다/아니다 (copula), past tense is 이었다/였다 (였다 after vowels) — 이었어요/였어요.
- Double past -았었/었었- can be used to emphasize that something happened in the more distant
  past and is no longer the case (e.g. 예뻤었어요 — "she used to be pretty [but isn't especially now]").
### Examples
* 어제 친구를 만났어요.
	* I met my friend yesterday.
* 아침에 밥을 먹었어요.
	* I ate breakfast this morning.
* 작년에는 학생이었어요.
	* Last year I was a student.
* 그 영화 정말 재미있었어요.
	* That movie was really fun.
### Notes
Future/presumptive is formed with -겠- (see [[~ 겠~]]) or -(으)ㄹ 거예요.
?end
