## (으)러 가다/오다 #card
?begin
### Usage
Expresses "go/come in order to do [verb]". Only used with movement verbs (가다, 오다, 다니다, 나가다, 들어가다, etc.) in the following clause.
### Form
| Verb stem ends in vowel | Verb stem ends in consonant |
| --- | --- |
| -러 | -으러 |
가다 → 가러 (rare, usually with other movement verbs), 먹다 → 먹으러, 사다 → 사러
### Restrictions
- The following clause MUST be a movement verb (가다/오다/다니다 etc.) — this is what distinguishes
  it from [[(으)려고]], which can be followed by any verb.
- Cannot attach to descriptive verbs (adjectives).
### Examples
* 밥을 먹으러 식당에 가요.
	* I'm going to the restaurant to eat.
* 친구를 만나러 카페에 왔어요.
	* I came to the cafe to meet a friend.
* 책을 사러 서점에 가요.
	* I'm going to the bookstore to buy a book.
* 운동하러 헬스장에 다녀요.
	* I go to the gym to exercise.
### Notes
Compare with [[(으)려고]] — 으러 requires a movement verb after it, while 으려고 can be followed by
any verb, including another movement verb or an unrelated one.
?end
