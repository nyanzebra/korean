## (으)로 #card
?begin
### Usage
A multi-purpose particle with several core meanings:
1. Direction ("toward")
2. Tool/means/method ("by means of, using")
3. Material ("made of")
4. Change/result ("into, resulting in")
5. Basic capacity/role (casual version of 로서)
### Form
| Ends in vowel or ㄹ | Ends in other consonant |
| --- | --- |
| 로 | 으로 |
### Restrictions
- With direction, often paired with movement verbs (가다, 오다, 향하다).
- For the more formal/nuanced "as a role/status" meaning, see [[로서 & 로써 & 로 인하여]].
### Examples
#### Direction
* 학교로 가요.
	* I'm going toward school.
#### Tool/means
* 젓가락으로 먹어요.
	* I eat with chopsticks.
* 버스로 왔어요.
	* I came by bus.
#### Material
* 이 책상은 나무로 만들었어요.
	* This desk is made of wood.
#### Change/result
* 신호가 빨간불로 바뀌었어요.
	* The signal changed to red.
#### Basic role/capacity
* 저는 학생으로 여기 왔어요.
	* I came here as a student.
### Notes
See [[로서 & 로써 & 로 인하여]] for the more formal/nuanced versions of the "as/by means of/due to"
meanings — plain (으)로 can substitute for all three in casual speech.
?end
