## 에 vs 에서 #card
?begin
### Usage
Both particles relate to location, but:
- **에** — a static location (where something/someone exists), a destination (where movement is directed), or a point in time.
- **에서** — the location where an action takes place (not just existing there, but doing something there), or the starting point of movement ("from").
### Form
Attaches directly to a noun. No vowel/consonant variation.
### Restrictions
- With 있다/없다 (existence), 에 is used: 집에 있어요 (I am at home).
- With action verbs (먹다, 공부하다, 만나다, etc.), 에서 is used: 집에서 밥을 먹어요 (I eat at home).
- 가다/오다 (movement) takes 에 for the destination: 학교에 가요 (I go to school).
### Examples
#### 에 — static location / destination / time
* 도서관에 있어요.
	* I am at the library.
* 학교에 가요.
	* I'm going to school.
* 세 시에 만나요.
	* Let's meet at 3 o'clock.
#### 에서 — location of action / starting point
* 도서관에서 공부해요.
	* I study at the library.
* 회사에서 집까지 멀어요.
	* It's far from the office to my house.
* 어디에서 왔어요?
	* Where are you from?
### Notes
A quick test: if you could insert "doing something" at that location, use 에서. If it's just
existing/appearing/being a destination, use 에.
?end
