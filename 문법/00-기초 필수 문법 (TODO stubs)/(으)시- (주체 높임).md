## (으)시- (subject honorific) #card
?begin
### Usage
The core subject-honorific infix, inserted into the verb/adjective stem to show respect toward the
subject of the sentence (typically someone older, of higher status, or a stranger being addressed
politely in the third person — e.g. a customer, teacher, grandparent).
### Form
| Stem ends in vowel or ㄹ | Ends in other consonant |
| --- | --- |
| -시- | -으시- |
가다 → 가시다/가세요, 읽다 → 읽으시다/읽으세요, 살다 → 사시다 (ㄹ drops before 시)

Some verbs have irregular honorific-specific forms instead of simply adding 시:
- 먹다/마시다 → 드시다/잡수시다 (eat/drink)
- 있다 (existence, about a person) → 계시다
- 자다 → 주무시다
- 죽다 → 돌아가시다
- 말하다 → 말씀하시다
### Restrictions
- Only used to honor the *subject*, not the speaker themselves (never use 시 to describe your own actions).
- When the honored person is the subject of a sentence with 이/가, use 께서 instead of 이/가, and
  에게 becomes 께 (다 대상): 선생님께서 오셨어요.
- Irregular honorific verbs (드시다, 계시다, 주무시다, 돌아가시다) replace the -시- infix entirely
  rather than combining with it.
### Examples
* 할머니께서 신문을 읽으세요.
	* Grandmother is reading the newspaper.
* 선생님, 어디 가세요?
	* Teacher, where are you going?
* 사장님은 지금 회의 중이세요.
	* The boss is currently in a meeting.
* 아버지께서 진지를 드세요.
	* Father is eating a meal. (드시다 = honorific "eat"; 진지 = honorific "meal")
* 할아버지께서 주무시고 계세요.
	* Grandfather is sleeping.

?end
