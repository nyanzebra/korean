## 밖에 #card
?begin
### Usage
"Only/nothing but" — used to emphasize that there is no other option or amount besides what's stated.
Unlike 만 (which is neutral "only"), 밖에 must be followed by a negative predicate, and it often
carries a nuance of insufficiency or regret ("there's only this much... and that's not enough").
### Form
Noun + 밖에 + (negative verb/adjective)
### Restrictions
- MUST be followed by a negative form (안, 못, 없다, 모르다, 아니다, etc.). "N밖에 있어요" is ungrammatical.
- Compare with [[만 vs 뿐]] — 만 can be used with positive predicates, 밖에 cannot.
### Examples
* 저는 한국어밖에 몰라요.
	* I only know Korean (and nothing else).
* 지갑에 돈이 천 원밖에 없어요.
	* I only have 1,000 won in my wallet (and that's not much).
* 한 명밖에 안 왔어요.
	* Only one person came.
* 이 방법밖에 없어요.
	* There's no other way but this.
### Notes
밖에 literally comes from 밖 (outside) + 에 (at) — "outside of X, there is nothing," which is why it
forces a negative predicate.
?end
