## 아/어 보다 #card
?begin
### Usage
Attached to a verb stem, expresses "to try doing [verb]" or "to have the experience of doing
[verb]". Softens a suggestion or invites someone to attempt something.
### Form
| Verb stem vowel | Ending |
| --- | --- |
| ㅏ, ㅗ | -아 보다 |
| other vowels | -어 보다 |
| 하다 | -해 보다 |
### Restrictions
- Only used with action verbs.
- In the past tense (-아/어 봤어요), it specifically emphasizes "I have tried/experienced doing X
  (before)" rather than simply "I did X."
- Often combined with -(으)세요 to make a gentle suggestion: -아/어 보세요 ("try doing...").
### Examples
* 이 음식 한번 먹어 보세요.
	* Try this food once.
* 제주도에 가 봤어요?
	* Have you ever been to Jeju Island? (tried going)
* 이 옷을 입어 볼게요.
	* I'll try on these clothes.
* 한국 드라마를 본 적은 없지만 한번 봐 보고 싶어요.
	* I've never watched a Korean drama, but I'd like to try watching one.
* 직접 만들어 보니까 생각보다 어려웠어요.
	* Now that I've tried making it myself, it was harder than I thought.
### Notes
This is the "exploratory auxiliary verb" briefly mentioned in [[Auxiliary Verbs]] — this card gives
it a dedicated, fuller treatment. Also compare the related form
[[다(가) 보니(까), 고 보니, 아 보니(까), 다(가) 보면, 고 보면]], which builds on 아/어 보다's stem to
express "now that I've done X, I realize/find...".
?end
