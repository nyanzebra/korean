## 아야/어야 하다(되다) #card
?begin
### Usage
Expresses obligation or necessity — "must do" / "have to do". 하다 and 되다 are largely
interchangeable, though 되다 can feel slightly more like "it is required/permitted that..." while
하다 feels more like an internal obligation.
### Form
| Verb/adj. stem vowel | Ending |
| --- | --- |
| ㅏ, ㅗ | -아야 하다/되다 |
| other vowels | -어야 하다/되다 |
| 하다 | -해야 하다/되다 |
### Restrictions
- Negative obligation ("must not") is NOT formed by simply negating 하다/되다 — instead use
  -(으)면 안 되다 ("must not / shouldn't").
- Both descriptive verbs and action verbs can attach.
### Examples
* 내일까지 숙제를 끝내야 해요.
	* I have to finish the homework by tomorrow.
* 건강하려면 운동해야 돼요.
	* To be healthy, you have to exercise.
* 이 일은 오늘 꼭 해야 됩니다.
	* This work absolutely must be done today.
* 시험을 보려면 여권이 있어야 해요.
	* To take the exam, you need to have a passport.
### Notes
For "must not," see [[안 & 못 & 지 않다 (부정)]] and the separate pattern -(으)면 안 되다 (not yet a
dedicated card in this vault — worth adding).
?end
