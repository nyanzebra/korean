## 는 동안 #card
?begin
### Usage
Expresses "while/during" a period of time — the second clause's action/state occurs within the
timeframe of the first clause's action. Can also attach directly to a duration noun (N 동안 = "for
a period of N").
### Form
| Pattern | Form |
| --- | --- |
| Verb + 는 동안 | Present-tense modifier + 동안 (동사에만 적용, no separate past form — tense of
  the whole event is expressed in the main clause) |
| Noun + 동안 | N + 동안 |

가다 → 가는 동안, 공부하다 → 공부하는 동안
### Restrictions
- Only used with action verbs for the "-는 동안" form (not descriptive verbs/adjectives).
- The subjects of the two clauses can be the same or different (unlike some other simultaneity
  connectives).
### Examples
* 제가 요리하는 동안 아이는 텔레비전을 봤어요.
	* While I was cooking, my child watched TV.
* 방학 동안 무엇을 했어요?
	* What did you do during the vacation? (N 동안)
* 제가 자는 동안 눈이 많이 왔어요.
	* While I was sleeping, it snowed a lot.
* 3년 동안 한국어를 공부했어요.
	* I studied Korean for 3 years. (duration)
### Notes
Compare with [[는 채로, 며, 면서, 을때|채(로)]] (see the 며/면서/을 때 comparison appended there) —
는 동안 emphasizes an entire span of time as the "container" for the other event, whereas 면서
emphasizes two simultaneous actions by the same subject.
?end
