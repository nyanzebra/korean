## Why both versions exist

- **Merged files** give a comprehensive side-by-side comparison of related patterns —
  good for understanding nuance and contrast.
- **Individual files** are quick single-topic reference cards — good for targeted
  review or flashcard practice on just one pattern.

This duplication is intentional, not an organization mistake. No further cleanup
needed; keep both.
