## 부터 & 까지 #card
?begin
### Usage
- **부터** — "from/starting at" (a time or sequence starting point).
- **까지** — "until/up to" (a time, place, or sequence end point).
They are frequently paired: N부터 N까지 ("from N to N").
### Form
Attaches directly to a noun. No vowel/consonant variation.
### Restrictions
- 부터 is mostly used for time/sequence, not physical starting points (use 에서 for physical "from").
- 까지 can be used for both time and physical destination ("as far as/up to").
### Examples
* 아홉 시부터 여섯 시까지 일해요.
	* I work from 9 to 6.
* 처음부터 끝까지 다 읽었어요.
	* I read it all from beginning to end.
* 여기서부터 저기까지 뛰어가세요.
	* Run from here to there.
* 내일까지 숙제를 내야 해요.
	* I have to submit the homework by tomorrow.
* 서울부터 부산까지 기차로 세 시간 걸려요.
	* It takes 3 hours by train from Seoul to Busan.
### Notes
서 can be added for physical locations: 여기서부터 (starting from here), 어디에서부터 (from where).
?end
