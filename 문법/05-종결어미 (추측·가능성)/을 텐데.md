## 을 텐데 #card
?begin
### Usage
Used to express supposition about some future, even one that didn't happen. Like "I am worried you will walk a lot so wear sneakers" or "it would have been nice if X had happened"
### Form
### Restrictions
The first clause is always background info and the second clause is always some suggestion or advice or question
### Examples
- 비가 올 텐데 우산을 가져가세요.
	- It's probably going to rain, so take an umbrella.
- 배고플 텐데 어서 드세요.
	- You must be hungry, please eat quickly.
- 좀 더 일찍 왔으면 길이 안 막혔을 텐데.
	- If you had come a bit earlier, the traffic wouldn't have been so bad.
