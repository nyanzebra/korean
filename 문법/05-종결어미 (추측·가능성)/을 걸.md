## 을/ㄹ 걸 #card
?begin
### Usage
- Used to express regret about a past action (or inaction), meaning "I should have..." Informal, often said to oneself.
- With 요 attached (-(으)ㄹ걸요), it expresses a weak guess or supposition, "I guess/probably..."
### Form
V/A + -(으)ㄹ 걸 (그랬다) → regret
V/A + -(으)ㄹ걸요 → weak guess
### Restrictions
- The regret form is mostly used in casual speech, ending the sentence directly (no 요 attached), often with falling intonation.
- The guess form -(으)ㄹ걸요 is usually used when responding to someone else, and cannot easily be used about the speaker's own definite plans.
### Examples
- 늦잠 자지 말고 더 일찍 일어날 걸.
	- I should have woken up earlier instead of oversleeping.
- 그렇게 매울 줄 알았으면 안 먹을 걸.
	- If I had known it would be this spicy, I wouldn't have eaten it.
- **가:** 영화가 재미있었어요?
	**나:** 잘 모르겠어요. 아마 별로일걸요.
	- A: Was the movie good? B: I'm not sure. I guess it's probably not that great.
